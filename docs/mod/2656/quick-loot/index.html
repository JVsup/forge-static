Description Versions 7

Tired of manually picking through a scav’s pockets for ammo and meds? QuickLoot lets you grab exactly what you need with a single keypress.

Open a body or container, press your hotkey, and the mod automatically transfers matching items straight to your inventory. No dragging, no clicking, just loot and go.

Features

Auto Search

Quick sort sorts items only in the backpack from top to bottom (default: G)

Configurable hotkey (default: F)

Category filters - choose what to auto-loot: Ammo, Meds, Food & Drink, Barter items

High value filter - automatically grabs expensive items based on live flea market prices (will be expanded in a future addon)

All settings are available in the F12 BepInEx menu in-game.
