Dragon Den - Hitmarker

Adds hitmarkers, headshot markers, kill markers, damage numbers, and hit sounds. Fully configurable in color, size, fade time, and style.

Features and Usage

Features

  • Fully customizable hitmarkers for normal hits, headshots, and kills
  • Choose between Cross, X, or custom PNG images for each hit type
  • Separate colors for normal, headshot, armor, and kill markers
  • Adjustable size, opacity, and fade-out time
  • Optional pulse effect on headshots
  • Floating damage numbers with rise and fade animations
  • Configurable text templates using tokens: {flesh}, {armor} and {bp}
  • Choose where damage numbers appear: hit position or screen center
  • Optional background behind damage numbers for better visibility
  • Detects and colors armor hits, ricochets, and blocked shots
  • Plays separate hit and kill sounds with individual volume and file settings
  • Master volume control and automatic reload when changing sound files
  • Customizable lifetime for hitmarkers and numbers
  • Debug mode logs hits, body parts, and weapon info to BepInEx console
  • All settings configurable directly through BepInEx menu

What is Dragon Den

Dragon Den is my twist on Tarkov. It’s where cuteness, edgyness, and weirdness all collide, while still keeping things believable.

  • Traders with their own quirks and attitude
  • Custom items and quests that feel fresh
  • Backpacks, armor, facewear and headwear with personality
  • Odd creations that shouldn’t exist… but somehow fit right in

No weapons, just everything else that makes Tarkov less boring.
