Don't Shoot The Bus

PMC bots keep picking fights with a 13-ton armored vehicle. This mod fixes that.

Chazut

Description Versions 1

Ever wonder why your PMC buddies have a death wish? PMC bots look at a 13-ton armored personnel carrier and think “yeah, I can take him.” Spoiler: they can’t. The BTR fires back, and suddenly Streets of Tarkov turns into a warzone with you caught in the crossfire.

Dont Shoot The Bus gives PMC bots the survival instinct they clearly lack. They’ll walk right past the BTR without picking a fight, and the BTR won’t turn them into modern art on the pavement.

One file, no config, zero headaches. Install and enjoy.

Compatible with SAIN and other AI mods — this patch runs before any AI decision logic.
