Raid Review

A web-based post raid replay system of your SPT raids; detailed replay system with heatmap overlay, review kills, lootings, positional data, and just get a general understanding of what was going on in your raids!

To use this mod, make sure the SPT Server is running, play a few raids with the mod installed of course.

Once you’ve played a few raids, open the web client from in-game by hitting F5 or opening your browser, and going to http://127.0.0.1:7829.

Installation

Download your compatible version from the ‘versions’ tab.

Each particular version will have specific installation / upgrade notes, please refer to this before commenting!

Fika Install

Raid Review is compatible with Fika (both regular hosted and headless).

Regular Fika (host + clients)

  • Install Raid Review normally on the host’s machine (the player who creates the lobby)
  • The host records everything — all bots, all connected players, kills, loot, positions
  • Connected players don’t need Raid Review installed

Fika Headless

  • Install Raid Review on the headless client only
  • The headless records all bots and connected human players
  • Since the headless has no “main player”, the raid type will always show as PMC
  • All connected players appear as USEC/BEAR PMCs in the leaderboard (the mod can’t distinguish them from AI PMCs)
  • Everything else works normally: map replay, kills, loot, bot behavior, charts

Note: Only install on ONE machine — either the host or the headless, not both.

Leaderboard

The post-raid leaderboard gives you a full breakdown of everyone in the raid at a glance.

  • Faction badges — Color-coded side column (USEC, BEAR, Scav, Raider, etc.) with a “Player” badge for you
  • Status column — KIA or Alive, no ambiguity
  • Sortable columns — Click to sort by Level, Kills, Looted items, or Accuracy
  • Accuracy bars — Mini progress bars color-coded green/yellow/red
  • Kill relationships — Click any row to highlight their victims (green) and killer (red); dead players show “killed by X (weapon, distance, bodyPart)”
  • Loot value — “Value” column shows total handbook price of items looted; “Spawn Val.” shows bot gear value at spawn
  • Your row highlighted — Gold left border accent so you always find yourself

Map Replay

Replay the entire raid on an interactive map with full positional tracking.

  • Animated replay — Watch every player and bot move in real-time with a timeline scrubber
  • Follow mode — Click any player in the legend to lock the camera on them
  • Map layers — Toggle between different floor levels for multi-story maps
  • Player tooltips — Hover any dot or legend entry for player info, faction, and level
  • Timeline events — Click events in the timeline bar to jump to that moment
  • BTR tracking — BTR shown with a dedicated APC silhouette icon

Kill Visualization

See exactly who killed who, where, and when — all on the map.

  • Kill lines — Hover a player to draw red dashed lines from killer to each victim with skull icons
  • Death markers — Dead players appear at their death position on hover with a killfeed tooltip
  • Legend highlights — Victims glow green, the killer glows red in the legend panel
  • Killfeed tooltip — Shows weapon used, distance, body part hit, and full kill/death history
  • Timeline-aware — Kill lines only show for events that happened before the current timeline position

Charts & Stats

Dive into raid analytics with interactive charts and detailed tooltips.

  • Active Bots Over Time — Line chart with faction breakdown in tooltip
  • Kills Over Time — Each data point shows a killfeed-style tooltip (attacker → victim, weapon)
  • Looting Over Time — Tooltip shows who looted what item
  • Projectiles Over Time — Tooltip shows shooter and weapon
  • Bots By Faction — Pie chart with player names on hover
  • PMC Personalities — Pie chart of SAIN personality distribution (requires SAIN)
  • Bots By Difficulty — Pie chart of bot difficulty levels
  • Color-coded timeline — Red for kills, orange for drops, green for looting events

Bot Behavior

See what every bot was thinking during the raid. Works best with SAIN installed, but supports vanilla AI too.

  • Behavior rings — Colored rings around bot markers on the map showing their current AI state
  • 10 behavior categories — Combat (red), Search (orange), Movement (blue), Patrol (gray), Medical (pink), Loot (yellow), Flee (purple), Extract (green), Grenade (dark orange), Other
  • Behavior tooltips — Hover any bot to see their current decision in the map tooltip
  • Gantt timeline — Dedicated /behavior page with horizontal timeline bars per bot
  • Zoom & scroll — Scroll-to-zoom on the Gantt chart, draggable scrollbar for navigation
  • Spawn/death indicators — Hatched bars before spawn and after death
  • Idle vs Patrol — Granular peace-mode tracking distinguishes standing still from patrolling
  • SAIN deep integration — Reads CurrentCombatDecision, CurrentSelfDecision, CurrentSquadDecision, and ActiveLayer via reflection
  • Vanilla fallback — Uses LastDecision when SAIN is not installed

Loose Loot

Visualize every piece of loot that spawned in the raid — on the ground and inside containers.

  • Map overlay — Thousands of loot markers rendered as lightweight SVG circles, color-coded by value:
    • Gray (<10K), White (10-50K), Blue (50-100K), Purple (100-500K), Gold (>500K)
  • Ground items — Diamond-shaped markers at exact spawn position
  • Container contents — Grouped markers showing all items inside a container in one tooltip
  • Dropped items — Dashed markers appear when players/bots drop items, disappear when picked back up
  • Price filter — Slider to set minimum value threshold (default 50K₽)
  • Sidebar panel — Sortable item list with search, price filter, ground/container toggle
  • Hover to locate — Hover any item in the sidebar to highlight it on the map with a tooltip
  • Click to pan — Click an item in the list to center the map on its position

Bot Inventory

Inspect what gear any bot spawned with.

  • Inventory panel — Collapsible section in the map sidebar with a bot dropdown selector
  • Slot grouping — Items organized by equipment slot: Weapons, Armor, Backpack, TacticalVest, Pockets, etc.
  • Weapon attachments — Shown as indented sub-items under each weapon
  • Item details — Name, quantity, and handbook price for every item
  • Total value — Spawn value sum displayed at the top of the panel

Supported Mods

Raid Review unlocks extra visualizations when these mods are installed. None are required — install only what you use.

  • See each squad’s long-term goals on the map (loot a rich area, hunt PvP, run a quest)
  • Bot tooltips tell you why a bot is heading somewhere or extracting
  • Click a bot to see its whole squad’s plan

Phobos

QuestingBots

LootingBots

Faction mods — RUAF Come Home, UNTAR Go Home, Black Division

Performance pass + map replay polish.

⚡ Performance

A big overhaul of how raid data is written to disk, aimed mainly at folks who saw stutter during raids:

  • No more per-line file writes. The position stream used to open, write and close the CSV file once per line — hundreds of times a second in a busy raid. It now keeps the file open and flushes on a timer, which kills the stutter on network drives and write-through RAID arrays.
  • SQLite is much lighter under load. WAL mode, connection pooling and batched inserts mean incoming data no longer queues up behind a single write lock. High fire-rate firefights (one row per shot) are now buffered and written in the background.
  • Raid pages load faster the more raids you keep. Added database indexes so the detail views stop scanning every table from top to bottom.

🗺️ Map Replay

  • Tooltip flicker is fixed. Hovering a bot, item, objective or loot tooltip while the replay is playing no longer strobes.
  • The followed player’s name no longer jitters. It used to wobble a pixel or two while following an entity; it’s stable now.
  • Speed multipliers actually work. 16x used to cap out at whatever your machine could render. Playback now skips frames at high speed, so 16x really is 16x.

🤖 New Faction

  • ISB faction support. ISB bots now show up as their own faction (own colour, marker and roles) instead of all being lumped in as “Follower”.

🐛 Fixes

  • ORBIT integration no longer floods the server log with thousands of “API ready” lines every raid.
