Overview

Introducing Better than QBZ-97? — a by-product of the original QBZ-97 project. Imported from the fictional firearms manufacturer Defense Group in Call of Duty, this rifle offers a QBZ-97 replica with enhanced customization options and a fire mode upgrade to full-auto.

Key Features:

- More customization options than the original QBZ-97.

- Firemode upgraded to full-auto for maximum effectiveness.

- Find it and its parts at Mechanic’s shop or on the Flea Market

Models and textures are properties of Activision.

Setup and Usage

1. Unzip the downloaded files.

2. Drop the mod folder in to the spt directory.

3. Launch the game.

4. Visit Mechanic’s shop or the Flea Market to find the rifle and its parts.
