PAUSE

This mod allows you to PAUSE the raid in progress, giving you time to take care of real life!

netVnum

Description Versions 3

This mod will pause the following:

  • You
    • Character movement
    • Health
    • Hydration and Energy
    • Stamina
  • AI
  • Time of day
  • Raid Timer will read “PAUSED”

Default keybinding is F9, but can be changed in the F12 BepInEx menu.

Possible Bugs, Conflicts, and Unexpected Behaviors:

Note: These may be due to issues with EFT code, and not the fault of SPT or this mod. There’s no guarantee that these can be fixed in future updates!

Note: None of the below have been reported as wide spread issues. I have been unable to replicate any of them:

  1. Pausing with less than 10 minutes on Lighthouse does not stop the timers in the background. Resuming the game will show you have lost time, potentially resulting in an early MIA.
  2. Adding extra time to raids using SVM or Raid Overhaul can interfere with pausing raid timers, potentially resulting in an early MIA.
  3. Pausing the game, pressing ESC to access the menus and then back into the game can stop the player from being able to loot and damage enemies when resumed.
  4. Pausing the game, pressing alt-tab to another window, returning to the game and resuming can stop the player from being to loot, interact, change weapons, or change stance.
  5. Pausing the game during a door opening or closing will stop the animation of the door. After resuming, the door is stuck in place and will no longer open or close.

Special thanks to everyone that has updated and released this mod in previous versions. Those of us like myself with interruptions Thank You!
