Description Versions 2

A follow up to my subsonic mod, this mod allows for truly end game performance in smaller calibers and also introduces a hypersonic 9x39 cartridge. This ammo is OP, so I tried to balance it with high recoil stats, high wear and heat stats, and high price locked behind level 3/4 traders. Please keep in mind this was made so I can LARP with a base makarov in labs and actually manage to down PMCs semi-reliably with APBS Bots with class 5 and 6 plates/helmets alongside my own buffed armor system (which is NOT in this mod), so yes I know the ammo is not exactly the most balanced but now you too can LARP with a PB like you’re secret agent in Tarkov, and not instantly die. What IS in this mod so far is:

  • 9x18 “Konchik Igly”, Level 3 Prapor
  • 9x19 Over Pressure AP, Level 4 Mechanic
  • 9x21 Model 6, Level 4 Peacekeeper
  • 9x39 Mechanic Special, Level 4 Mechanic
  • 45 ACP Hyper Pen, Level 3 Peacekeeper
  • 50 AE Anti Armored Asset, Level 4 Peacekeeper
  • 57x28 Project S, Level 4 Peacekeeper
  • 357 Mag Maximum, Level 4 Skier
  • 762x25TT AP Flat Nose, Level 3 Prapor
  • 12/70 Anti-Material Slug, Level 4 Skier
  • 20/70 Anti-Material Slug, Level 4 Prapor
  • 12.7x55mm PS12Ax, Level 4 Prapor

As far as I saw, no bots have used the ammo against me in gameplay testing.

Most Recent WTT Common Lib required for the mod to work - Also, has no manifest. Do not know what that is, doesn’t affect my gameplay beyond some scary server text. Download at your own risk, I tested it and it works for me but as I say this mod is optional.
