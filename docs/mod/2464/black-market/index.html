FEATURES

  • Robust and documented config

Fence

Assort
  • Uses a whitelist system to only offer items chosen by the player.
  • By default, Fence will offer highly coveted items, making it worthwhile to frequent his assort.
  • By default, the whitelist supports the majority of custom items and equipment such as those found in WTT Armory, Massivesoft, Epic’s AIO, and more.
Questline
  • 67 handwritten quests with photoshopped art–no AI used.
  • Designed for a zero-to-hero playthrough.
  • Fence assort, flea market buying, and flea market selling all progressively unlocked through the questline.
  • +2.5 Fence rep from entire questline.
  • Supports Quest Extended for increased quest objective variety. Hat tip to Light of the World!
  • Walkthrough coming soon.

Flea Market

Buying
  • Complete flea market offer rework. All items that can accept attachments can be tastefully randomized.
  • Dynamic offer creation to keep the market feeling alive.
  • Full control over generated offer fundamentals.
  • As the generations are dynamic, support for current and future custom items is included.
Selling
  • Full control over offer count and reputation.

Hideout

Crafting
  • Custom and seasonal item crafting.
Scav Case
  • Reconfigured to accept only cigarettes, alcohol, and valuable information items.
