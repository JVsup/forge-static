Work in progress voice mod.

This mod adds a new voice, Ghost, voiced by Craig Fairbrass. Lines were taken from Modern Warfare 2 (2009) and Call of Duty 4 (Gaz). All lines are clean, genuine recordings. No AI.

Currently up to 151 voice lines including line variations. https://youtu.be/f7bCuRnfccQ

I recommend Smooth Talker, as one of my goals will be to give those voice lines as much variety as possible. https://forge.sp-tarkov.com/mod/2635/smooth-talker

You can edit the bot types this voice may be chosen for by editing the config in \SPT\user\mods\Ghost Voice\db\CustomVoices\GhostVoice.json

Here is a list of all the bots: https://wiki.sp-tarkov.com/en/modding/references/bot-types

Check against voice weights for each bot type in \SPT\SPT_Data\database\bots\types\
