Description

Be A cowboy, in a place and time where everything is currently burning down. Only one will uphold the law.

The Colt Single Action Revolver is now in player hand. Enjoy waiting for 5 years while you unload and load this thing

Weapon and its related item can be obtained from Skier LL3 (Weapon is barter, Ammo and Magazine is roubles). Can also be found in Reserve, Factory and Woods (place is secret). Also spawns in safe, good luck finding the ammo lol. Can be bought from flea.

Remember, engraving give you no tactical advantage whatsoever.

Install Instructions

1. Download the Zip from the Gdrive

2. Open the Zip

3. Copy the content of the Zip your SPT folder

4. Enjoy

Known Issue

Due to how Big Silly Goose handle empty reload for the revolver, I could not make single ejection work without a client modification. As a work around if you have 2 shells and 0 live round, the gun will eject on all chamber playing a sound. This is a much better alternative than the gun ejecting all shells after ejecting one. I’ll try to revisit the single ejection mechanic but honestly Big Silly Goose’s really makes me cry.
