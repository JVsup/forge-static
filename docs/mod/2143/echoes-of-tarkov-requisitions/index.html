Overview

Echoes of THE CITY – Requisitions

is the ITEMS ONLY preview version of the larger Echoes of THE CITY mod. This standalone pack introduces a wide array of new weapons, suppressors, gear, cosmetics, and utility items to enrich your Escape from THE CITY experience within SPT. It includes content like the UMP 9, Pit Viper pistol, armor and ammo boxes, custom handguards, suppressors, and unique apparel—offering a taste of what’s to come in the full story-driven release.

Echoes of THE CITY

What is

Echoes of THE CITY is a gritty, story-driven mod that plunges players into the chaos left behind after a brutal power shift in the Norvinsk region. With old leaders dead, a new order rises from the ashes. You play as Rook — wounded, stripped of everything, and left for dead — now guided by a mysterious new figure known only as Viper.

In this unforgiving version of THE CITY, every choice echoes.

This isn’t just another run.
This is the beginning of something deeper.

Echoes of THE CITY is a lore and gameplay expansion of Escape From THE CITY. Echoes of THE CITY aims to add new story to the world of Escape from THE CITY. This will explain alot of weird inconsistencies, for example, why the PMC is dirt broke despite the game taking 4-17 years after the blockade of THE CITY was put in place. Echoes of THE CITY currently aims to include three new traders, although more are in a concept phase. With the mod added to your game, it will rebalance the early stages of THE CITY by locking all traders (Except Viper and Fence) and adding new quests to unlock them 1 by 1. As previously stated, every choice echoes, there will be butterfly effects based on your choices and actions. Some quests will be fail-able without re-dos. Echoes of THE CITY will include custom items, such as new clothing, clothing items, Guns, Attachments, etc.
