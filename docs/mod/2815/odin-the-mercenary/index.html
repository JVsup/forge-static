ABOUT THE MOD

ODIN - THE MERCENARY is a mod that brings the legendary mercenary back into the game to make life difficult for you. What’s more, this mod includes exclusive items that are only available on ODIN. Before starting a raid, you’ll receive a notification in the bottom right-hand corner if ODIN spawns in your round.

INSTALLATION
  1. Back up your profile before every update!!!
  2. Delete every previous version completely!!!
  3. Clear your caches!!!
  4. Download the mod!!!
  5. Extract the ZIP!!!
  6. Copy the contents of the ZIP into your SPT root folder!!!
UNINSTALLATION

Step 1: Go to: SPT\BepInEx\patchers and delete TheMercenaryPrepatch.dll

Step 2: Go to: SPT\BepInEx\plugins and delete the folder TheMercenary

Step 3: Go to: SPT\SPT\user\mods and delete the folder TheMercenary aswell

WEB UI

First start the server, then open your browser at http://127.0.0.1:6970/ to access the configuration options. Click “Save & Apply” after every change.

ABOUT ODIN

  • HUNT MODE
    If you’re a truly hardened, no-nonsense bastard, press F12 in-game to activate Hunt Mode. ODIN will switch to Berserker Mode and hunt you down until you croak.

  • SAIN
    THE MERCENARY, like other bots, is fully configurable via SAIN.

MIMIMI

Ladies and gentlemen, or whatever you choose to identify as, I’m not interested in your whingeing about ODIN being too strong. In the past, ODIN was even stronger, and I’ve already backtracked to tone him down a bit. Only download this mod if you’re truly experienced and not afraid to die. Because you will. Often.
