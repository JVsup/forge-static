Boss Notifier

Notifies you of which bosses you'll face in your upcoming raid!

Description Versions 3
Basics

Turns your ordinary intelligence center into a boss detector!

Default hotkey to show notifications is ‘O’

Various details are unlocked by the level of your intel center.

Boss Notification Details:

- Base information includes whether or not a boss will be in your raid.

- Location detail will notify you of the bosses spawn zone (where on the map they’re gonna spawn)

- Detection checkmark notifies you of which bosses have already spawned in your raid (Look for a checkmark next to the notification)

Boss Detection Feature:

- Notifies you when a boss has been detected in your vicinity.

Key differences between regular boss notifications and boss detections:

The boss notifications only show information about what bosses will spawn *in the future*

The detection notification happens when a boss is actually spawn into the game, meaning they’re somewhere on the map with you. My aim for this feature was to add some certainty to whether or not a boss is actually spawned in and active.

How To Install

Simply extract BossNotifier.zip into your SPT game folder.

How To Config

Hit F12 in game to open your config settings.

1. What intel center level you will unlock the functionality of this mod. If this is greater than your intel center level then you will not receive notifications.

2. What intel center level you need in order to receive location data on the bosses present in your raid.

3. What intel center level you need in order to receive notifications when a boss spawns near you.
