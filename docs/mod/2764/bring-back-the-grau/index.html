Overview

Bring Back the GRAU! is another shitpost mod I felt like making for fun. This one makes everything worse in every way for everyone and I’m not even remotely sorry.

What does it do?

This mod takes all (applicable) Russian Ammo and Grenades in Tarkov and renames it to follow the Russian GRAU and GAU indexes for military items.

If you want to even slightly make sense of this mod, then the EFT Wiki’s GRAU Index Page will be very useful to you.
