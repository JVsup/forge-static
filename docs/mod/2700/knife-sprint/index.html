Description Versions 1

KnifeSprint gives you a small sprint speed boost whenever your melee weapon is out. Default is a subtle 15% enough to feel snappier without being broken. Fully configurable in BepInEx if you want more or less.

Switching back to your gun mid-sprint? Boost drops instantly, so no you cant cheese, or can you? :D

Config: Sprint Speed Multiplier default 1.15, range 1.0–2.0

All settings are available in the F12 BepInEx menu in-game.
