DESCRIPTION

Anastasia provides a wide range of weapon accessories!

Some examples of what she offers :

Svetlana, a BEAR soldier, plays a crucial role as a go-between for newcomers and the missions given to recruits. She is known for providing reliable, high-quality weapons, as well as weapons customized to meet the specific needs of the soldiers. The best part is that the prices are 20% cheaper than any other trader, and she can provide unlimited quantities of weapons if the work is of high quality. All in all, Svetlana is an indispensable resource for the BEARs and their mission.

Some examples of what she offers :

EVELYN, a seasoned USEC veteran and adept entrepreneur in Tarkov, leads the charge to reclaim Terragroup assets, rallying fellow operatives with a 15% exclusive discount. Her mission is to expose secrets and seek revenge for their abandoned main command after the contractors’ war.
