Description Versions 1

DEPENDENCIES

-> WTT CommonLib 1.0.0

DISCLAIMER

This is a fork of kyoukopan’s Custom First Aid Kits

  • Creates separate medkits that act as containers.

NEW FEATURES

Medkits
  • The fillable medkits now have custom grid layouts to represent the physical shape of the container.
Medical Items
  • Adds new medical items that administer transfusions or injections to replace traditional medkits with full body healing.
  • Reworks existing medical items to offer different functionality or balance.

Most notably:

  • Augmentin now buffs Surgery skill at the cost of Metabolism.
  • Goldenstar now “wakes you up” with a large list of attention-focused buffs.
  • Analgesic painkillers now have a small chance to cause side effects.

HIGHLY RECOMMENDED MODS

Merge Consumables 1.3.1

Rarities

CONFLICTING MODS

As this is a near-total overhaul of all medical items, it is not recommended to use other mods that also affect the medical items, i.e. Realism’s medical changes.
