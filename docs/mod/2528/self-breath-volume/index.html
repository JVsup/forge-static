Self Breath Volume

Modify the breath volume & hurt vocal of yourself (Using mask, low stamina, get injured …)

Description Versions 2

Change the volume percent of player’s sound (only yourself) by:

Two parameters for two types of sounds:

Breath Volume 0-100%:
OnBreath (faceshield / mask)
Dehydrated
Exhausted

Hurt Vocal Volume 0-100%:
Bleeding, Hit, HurtLight, HurtMedium, HurtHeavy, HurtNearDeath, LegBroken, OnAgony, OnBeingHurt, OnBeingHurtDissapoinment.
(These are names of sound effect types in EFT client)

How to install or uninstall

Download the ZIP, open and drag the BepInEx folder to your SPT main folder.
To uninstall, just remove BepInEx/plugins/SelfBreathVolume.dll.
