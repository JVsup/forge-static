Description Versions 1

About:

THE CITY’s Base armor inserts currently make some armor genuinely not worth using, this mod aims to provide reason to utilize different armor pieces whilst maintaining some sort of hierarchy. If it is large/rare in base THE CITY, expect it to be noticeably buffed in this mod.

Install Download, un-zip using 7zip into main SPT directory

To uninstall just delete the mod from the >mods folder

Yes both you and the bots are affected. Expect boss equipment to be buffed and the same for heavily armored (with vanilla equipment) enemies, that Zabralo wearing chad got a whole lot scarier and so did Killa and Tagilla.

Highly recommend using with the Unrestricted Armor Plate mod on the forge so you can swap out the armor inserts as needed and so you can use whatever plate with whatever vest to really explore the new strengths and weaknesses of these armors.

NOTE:

No manifest, do not know what that is but other than some server text, you will be fine. Or should be IMOE.

  • Also, ODTItem info does not really reflect the new material or name changes, so the inserts will be named “Aramid insert 3/X” for the most part but the math on effective durability and class still works in my experience. And of course in game performance still follows.

Again this is mainly a buff, be aware. Changes in README on the github.

Initial Release:

  • Most Body Armors and Armored Vests have had their inserts altered and buffed (I have always disliked how like mid-high tier ammo essentially nullified armors of all kinds since inserts were capped at level 3 for most pieces or just level 4 for very low durability pieces.)
  • This affects you AND THE BOTS, expect bosses and heavily armored bots to be much tankier now Preview of other equipment to be altered included such as the maska and neosteel kit ;)
  • I HIGHLY recommend using the Armor Insert mod from the forge to mix and match pieces for an extra element of armor customization (I mean we have sewing kits in game, why not rip out armor from one vest and sew it onto another for a personalized armored kit?)
  • This mod alters blunt through put of armors and their plates based on material (so yes armor plates are altered slightly)\

  • New Blunt through put values, low durability and low repair usually means less damage allowed through (Prior to breaking or pen) to make up for those factors and vice versa, now ceramic plates aren’t straight up garbage! (aluminium is Aluminium Oxide… which should be a ceramic so I made it similar blunt through put but decided against altering the indestructibility stat of it for now)

  • Ceramic - 0 (Nearly no damage through until broken or pen)

  • Glass - 0

  • Aluminium - 0.015

  • ArmoredSteel - 0.03

  • Combined - 0.05

  • Titan - 0.075

  • UHMWPE - 0.15

  • Aramid - 0.2 (20% blunt damage through until broken or pen)

  • These new values are still buffs compared to base but, now normalized rather than a per piece basis to allow easier player decision making
