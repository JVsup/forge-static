Description Versions 3

Slims down your magazines to fit in your nice little pockets. 2x2 are now 1x2, 1x3 are now 1x2, and guns no longer get an extra size down penalty from magazines.

NOTE: EFT caches your item icons. If something looks off, press the “Clean Temp Files” button in your SPT launcher to fix it.
