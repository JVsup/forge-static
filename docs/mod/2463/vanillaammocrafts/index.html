!!!!PAY ATTENTION TO HOW TO INSTALL!!!!

  1. Download Zip 2. Unzip 3. Place “SPT” folder directly where you have SPT installed so it should be:

SPT directory > SPT > user > mods > VanillaCraftAmmo folder (not the first folder, the one inside the folder named “mods”)

To uninstall just delete the mod from your mods folder, thats it

Tarkov has a crafting system but like none of the ammo crafts are worth your time and the ones that ARE worth it aren’t unlocked until you 100% NG+++ no death secret ending romance lightkeeper and take 40 hidden transit extracts in one raid. This mod remedies that, expect to be able to start from humble hollow points or low tier ammo and craft your way to Igolnik, MAI, .338 AP. and EVEN .50 BMG by doing what a PMC in Tarkov does best! Which is ratting for supplies and grinding through the gear hierarchy. Just know, when making an omelet you will break a few eggs… IE don’t always expect a 1:1 ratio of input to output, your pmc isn’t perfect at doing this and messes up sometimes losing a few rounds in the process.

Basic info - All located in Workbench Levels 1 (up to around 30ish pen), Level 2 (up to 40+ ish pen), and Level 3 (top tier ammos) all you need the traders for is a constant supply of 5.45 US if you must or just find it in the world.

General Upgrade Paths:

Screwdriver = Punch a hole in the bullet to make it a bigger hollow point or something Multi-Tool = Enhance the bullet using the various tools or perhaps hand sharpen them -> slight improvements Wires = melted down to create copper jackets to make FMJ ammo (stage 1 ammo improvement) Screws = inserted into bullets to make mild AP inserts (stage 2 ammo improvement) Nails = inserted into bullets to make Stronger AP inserts (stage 3 ammo improvement) Metal parts = rare metals used to make sabots and high end AP inserts (stage 4 ammo improvements)

Some cartridges are used to make others****

US/Subsonic AP type bullets = have tungsten in them so they are used as special ingredients to improve or make other AP ammos.

Also yes you can deconstruct certain ammos for materials if need be.

Listen, I had no issues with this mod with or without other mods but it WILL make opening the workbench take a while since its 90+ crafts. Be warned, If any issues arise I wont be able to fix it cause im not a coder im just straight groggin it. Your Risk Downloading this mod, again you have been warned. Also I threw in an F1 grenade craft to the shortened F1 grenades partizan uses, have fun with that!

Also short tip, if you are going no flea/trader I highly recommend Hoarding 5.45 US… you’ll need it.
