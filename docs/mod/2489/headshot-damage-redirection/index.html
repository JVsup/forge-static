Headshot Damage Redirection

Survive headshots by redirecting damage to other body parts. Fully configurable split ratios, thresholds, and RNG options. Stop getting one-tapped and fight back! Compatible with SPT 4.0.7 & FIKA

What does it do?

Tired of getting instantly killed by headshots? This mod splits incoming head/chest damage between multiple body parts, giving you a chance to survive and fight back.

Default behavior (60% redirect):

  • Take a 100 damage headshot
  • Head takes: 40 damage
  • Other body parts: 60 damage (split)
  • You survive to tell the tale!

Key Features

Fully Configurable - Adjust redirect percentage, damage thresholds, body part targets Chest Protection - Optional chest damage redirection Smart Thresholds - Ignore low-damage hits, cap maximum damage RNG Mode - Add chance-based redirection for unpredictability In-Game Feedback - See exactly how damage was redirected Dad Gamer Mode - Global damage reduction slider for casual play

Configuration (F12 in-game)

All settings accessible via BepInEx Configuration Manager:

  • Redirect percentage (0-100%)
  • Minimum damage threshold
  • Maximum damage cap
  • Chance to activate
  • Body part selection
  • And more!

Installation

  1. Download zip
  2. Extract in your SPT folder
  3. Launch game
  4. Press F12 to configure settings
