Description Versions 3

Introducing the most popular round around to the world of Tarkov!

The guns that use this ammo are currently all in my True North mod.

This mod adds the ‘versatile’ and widely-loved (or hated) .22 Long Rifle calibre.

Perfect for quiet Scav elimination, annoying PMC’s with any armor at all, and for when you completely run out of money and you need something that technically goes bang.

This pack serves as a foundational ammunition library, designed to be expanded upon by other weapon modders in the community. Features:

This mod includes a few ammunition types, with more to come:

-CCI .22 LR Standard Velocity,

-CCI Stinger .22LR Copper Hollowpoint

-CCI 22 LR Shotshell #12 Shot

-Piney Mountain .22LR Tracer

-Lapua SK Standard Plus

-Eley Sport

-Eley Tenex Biathlon

-CCI Hyperspeed

A Note for Mod Authors:

If you’d like to add .22 to any of your mods:

Add this mod as a hard dependency in your own project’s metadata.

Reference the item IDs for the ammunition directly in your weapon or magazine files.

Please do not repackage, integrate, or bundle the files from this mod with your own.

If you’d like to create more round types, please send them over to me and I will integrate them and give credit, or create an addon for this mod.
