What does this add?

This mod adds 3 new rounds to the game.

How do I use them?

Load em into any stanag mag that takes them and load that mag into the Adar.

Who sells them?

Prapor, Skier, and flea offers are ways you can buy them.

Lemme see em!

LOREEEEE!!!!!

The 5.56x50mmE round was designed by Viktor Mirovoy, also known as Арматура Строганов or Rebar Stroganoff.

Viktor was a Russian extremist who fought the government any way he could. Viktor was captured in the early 2000s, due to his ability to create makeshift munitions they assigned him to an experimental program. It was under this program that Viktor designed these three rounds specifically for the Adar rifle.

How do I install it?

Download the mod, open the zipped file, open “556x50mmE-(version)”, and drag the “user” file into your SPT directory!
