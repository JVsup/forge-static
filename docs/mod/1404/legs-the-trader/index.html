Legs the Trader

Description Versions 11

I was tired of buying fully built weapons and stripping them myself/filling my inventory with garbage mods. 30 quests that start after Gunsmith - Part 1 completion and reaching level 5 will slowly unlock more weapons platforms. First weapons are 5.45 AKs and Adar. After levelling trader and completing quests, things like m4a1, 7.62 AKs all the way to MCX Spear at the very end are unlocked for purchase.

2.1.0 Release - 4.0.0 Compatible.

NOTE: “Should” (Tested myself but if you are deep into a playthrough may be best to just wait) be safe to update existing profiles but going back to older versions = big NO NO. So only update if you plan to stick with 2.1.0+. Also the new guns re-use vanilla assets. One does use assets from another weapon so clipping is inevitable but weapon part selection was chosen to make it as minimal as possible. Speaking of the M4C9, While there is a small amount of clipping it does allow you to make weapons that look like the small Honeybadger.

Version Notes:

-Added 1 new Quest “Snap-on”

-Added 3 new Weapons M4C9, M754, M14 - Note: These do not work for vanilla quests.

-Reworked quest json. Updated with newer weapons and Fixed a bug in Dialed In 1&2

-Added RU Translation Credit: Sapskerly

-New Trader pic: Will have to clear temp files in spt launcher to see change.

I also wanted to say thanks to everyone in the community as its been awesome learning new things and helping others.

Dependency : WTT-CommonLib

Install: If you have WTT-CommonLib already installed just drag and drop into the root folder of SPT.

-This literally wont work without it.

Unlock: Completing Gunsmith 1 and reaching lvl 5 will grant access to his unlock quest titled: “A Friend in Need”.

lvl2: requires level 20 and some spending.

lvl3: requires level 25 and some more spending.

Max: requires level 30 and lots of spending.
