What’s Included

WTT-ContentBackport brings 1.0 content to SPT 4

This mod adds virtually EVERY new item, clothing, hideout customization, voice line, and character head from the 1.0 release. If you’ve been curious what the live version added, it’s all here.

Content Added:

  • All new weapons, attachments, and gear
  • Character customization (heads, clothing, voices)
  • Hideout upgrades and decorations
  • Barter items, food, and medical supplies
  • New ending environments + Miami environment
  • Custom WTT Achievements for unlocking some of the exclusive items
What’s Different from Live 1.0

We couldn’t backport everything—here’s what’s missing:

  • Live Achievements + Quest content. No main story.
  • New prestige levels UPDATE NOW THERE’S A MOD FOR THAT :0 https://forge.sp-tarkov.com/mod/2540/content-backport-prestiges#versions
  • Terminal, and any reworked maps (labs, interchange, ect)

What we did rebuild:

All automatic weapons were reconstructed from scratch to work properly in SPT. BIG shoutout to Eco and Eukyre for undertaking this project.

How to Unlock Content

Option 1: Achievements - We’ve added achievable unlock conditions for Twitch drops, preorder content, and other exclusive items. Earn them naturally as you play. You can see these achievements in your achievements tab, as well as the unlock conditions for each.

Option 2: Cheat Codes - Use Commando commands to unlock everything instantly. Open a chat with “Commando” in your friends list, type help to Commando to see all available commands. If there is no Commando in your friends list, you more than likely have Fika installed disabling Commando from being used. These achievements are HIDDEN, the ONLY way to see what they do is to use them. FAFO

Example command:

spt nikitasvoice

Unlocks Nikita’s voice for your faction (BEAR/USEC).

Note: Some unlocks require a game restart. Menu refresh is needed before achievements display on your character if they’re earned via cheats. We’re actively working on a solution to both issues.

What is WTT?

What is Welcome To Tarkov?

Welcome To Tarkov (WTT) is a mod for SPT AKI that has been in development for over a year. What started as a small passion project has grown in size to incorporate 15+ core members as well as contributions from other well-known mod authors and friends of WTT alike.

How does Welcome To Tarkov change the game?

As a mod, WTT overhauls SPT AKI, touching on almost every vanilla game system, as well as adding completely new systems to the game and its core mechanics. Inspired by open-world hardcore survival projects like STALKER: Anomaly and STALKER: GAMMA, WTT takes SPT’s artificial multiplayer MMO difficulty adders and replaces them with a challenging world economy, loot scarcity rebalancing, a lore friendly quest overhaul, and a map-traveling system built from rewritten portions of the Traveler mod allowing seamless travel between maps. Aside from its changes to the game’s core properties, WTT also adds to the world’s variety of traders, NPC’s, characters, items, and quests:

  • Over 1,000 custom bundles adding new weapons, attachments, wearables, containers, food, medical, and barter items.
  • New bosses with their own custom gear that will be properly incorporated into WTT’s quest lines.
  • New, meaningful traders with their own lore-friendly quests and narrative elements to help the player progress through the story-line of WTT.
  • New playable characters and outfits that will add to the player’s customization abilities.
  • New quest types incorporating brand new map extraction points, putting the player’s knowledge and familiarization of SPT’s maps to the test.

Welcome To Tarkov stands as one of the largest mods created for SPT AKI thus far.
