Continuous Healing

Tired of having to heal every limb one by one? Now you can heal them all in one go until done or manually cancelled!

What does the mod do?

With Continuous Healing you can click the healing button once and it will continue healing all limbs until you reach 100%, removing the need to do it for every limb and waiting for the animations to bring up your weapon. The delay that is normally 2 seconds is removed between each limb, speeding up healing slightly as well. If the med kit runs out it will stop as per usual. Tested and working with Fika.

I highly recommend using this with Healing Autocancel!

Incompatible mods

Better Meds Revised

Known issues

Healing a destroyed limb with a CMS or Surv12 kit will not play the animation on the second limb due to how the animations work for these items. You will still heal them at a slightly faster rate, just wait until your weapon appears and it’s done. Looking for an elegant fix if possible down the line.

If you have any issues with the mod, be thorough in your report or you will be ignored. Thanks!
