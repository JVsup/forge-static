🎄English

Overhaul of how Tarkov can be played

🧮 In-Game User Interface

For new players, after install:

  • Press F5 to open Game Manager, most of configure settings are there
  • Press F12 and click “JiangHu”, some features are there
  • Click “Description”, it has overall mod info
  • All detailed guides have 7 languages versions

👥 Universal Bot Generator

  • One-click to spawn/remove any bot at any time, on any map, with any hostility
  • Option to disable all default bot spawns: Play only with your own bot teammates, enemies, or neutrals

⚔️ Bot War & Death match

  • Click 「樂園」 button on the main menu to play alongside bot teammates, against multiple enemy bot teams, with your own bot roster (all bosses, PMCs, Rogue, etc), in death match mode, on all maps
  • Switch to free camera mode to observe the bot battles, or switch back to rejoin the fight
  • In free camera mode, select and place bots on the map, like arranging pieces on chessboard

🍂 Random Map + Randow Transit + Hardcore mode & Quest Type

  • Click 「江湖」 button on the main menu to play, select map(s) in F5 game manager
  • “Dance on the Razor’s Edge”: Energy & Hydration drain at 1 point every 10 seconds + all boss kill XP reduced to 1000
  • “Aporia” Quests: Reach high XP targets within a single raid while playing with both. rewards skill levels, can reset anytime to earn more levels

🪽 New movement & handling - Martial Arts

  • Instant Response, Clean & Smooth Movement
  • Faster walk/sprint, stance transitions, leaning
  • Sliding, Mid-Air Jump
  • Faster weapon switching / aiming, no aimpunch
  • Wider freelook
  • Stellar Transposition: Instantly swap positions with any bot you are looking at
  • Kung Fu Bot: As you master each martial art (in Physics), bots will learn and use it too

🤖 Bot Highlight & Marker

  • In normal raid, highlight & mark bots by side, makes them clearly visible: green for teammates, red for enemies, white for neutral
  • In Bot war & Death match, highlight & mark each team with unique color
  • Can be toggled on/off anytime

🌌 New UI

  • New main menu: rearrange buttons, add 2 new game mode buttons, removed “alpha warning”
  • Background Selector: Set your video as dynamic background, or image as static background
  • BGM Player: Set your music as BGM, can play in hideout and raid too
  • Jiang Hu Art DLC

🎋 Battle Screen

  • Show detailed damage stats after every hit: which body part was hit and took how much damage, which armor/helmet was hit and how much durability was lost, and how much damage absorbed by armor/helmet
  • Kill record for each bot type: Scav, PMC, Boss, etc
  • In death match: displays each team’s K/D
  • Freely move and place the battle screen anywhere on the screen
  • Scroll to view raid history, and Automatically saves a copy to a local log file in BepInEx\plugins\JiangHu.Client\fight_log

🔮 Dogtag Collection Quest Type

  • New bot names: select from myths, literature, folklore, movies, anime, etc
  • Collect dogtags with specific nicknames to complete quests and recieve rewards

🏯 New Items & Trader

  • 1×1 size containers that can hold ANYTHING, fits in ANY slot
  • in-raid repair kits
  • Other unique pristine supplies
  • New Trader Buys ALL items at handbook price with roubles, giving you the best value and showing the true base value of every item

🪄 Random Quest Generator

  • Play randomized vanilla questlines

☯️ New Quest

  • Jiang Hu Bounty: Eliminate ALL bosses in one single raid. This is the only path to Prestige. you may attempt it at any time, even at Level 1. If succeed, you will become a true Jiang Hu Da Xia and may prestige immediately
  • 9 Main Quests: replace the vanilla questline, focused and intensified experience
  • Reward all around skill levels. all skills can reach elite level in 1 play through
  • Cover all vanilla quest unlocks, and unlock them by category per quest, such as items, recipes, traders & standings, customization

🎮 New Game Settings

  • Flea Market locked
  • Early access to Kappa
  • Cash Wipe on Death: Lose 10% of total cash each death, with the first 1 million always protected
  • No Insurance: Traders stop offering insurance
  • Eventually traders stop doing any bussiness with you at all, you must rely entirely on loot
  • Your security container will be disabled
  • And more

🌸 Story mode & Free Mode

  • In Story mode, start your journey as a Young Wanderer, take quests from the legendary Long Gate Inn, grow stronger, learn rare martial arts, earn powerful artifacts, and face increasingly dangerous challenges on your path toward the ultimate Jiang Hu Bounty
  • Combines many features above — settings, quests, new movement systems, etc — to deliver a completely different gameplay experience
  • In Free Mode, All features can be toggled on or off. turn all of them off will return to vanilla game. you can customize and explore your own Jiang Hu experience
  • Recommended: use one profile for immersive story mode and one for free mode

📱 Install & Use

  • Just like all mods
  • If playing with an existing profile, Back up your profile first

塔科夫玩法的重造更新

🧮 游戏内用户界面

新手宝宝在安装模组后：

  • 按F5键打开游戏管理中心，大部分的设置都在那里
  • 按F12键点击“江湖”，那里也有一些重要功能
  • 点击“描述”，查看模组的综合介绍
  • 所有详细指南均有7国语言版本

👥 点将台

  • 一键召唤/移除任何人机，任意地图，任何时间，任何敌友关系
  • 可选禁止系统刷人机，只与自定义的队友、敌人或中立人机一起玩

⚔️ 乐园

  • 点击主菜单的「樂園」按钮，和人机队友一起，使用自定义的队伍阵容（所有头目、佣兵、反叛者等等），在所有地图上与多支人机队伍展开死斗混战
  • 可随时切换到自由视角观战，或切换回来加入战斗
  • 在自由视角下，可以选中并将人机放置在地图任何位置，就像下棋一样

🍂 全新模式与任务类型

  • 江湖行：随机地图 + 随机转图，点击主菜单的「江湖」按钮玩，在F5游戏管理器中选择地图
  • 惊鸿猎：能量与水分消耗调整为每10秒减少1点，击杀首领的经验统一下调至1000
  • 向阳而生：新任务类型，单次战局内达成高经验值目标，需开启“惊鸿猎”后点击“江湖”玩。奖励技能等级，可随时重置以获取更多等级

🪽 凌波微步

  • 瞬时响应、干净流畅的移动
  • 神行术：更快的行走/冲刺、姿态转换、侧身速度
  • 滑铲，半空跳跃
  • 更快的武器切换/瞄准，无瞄准受击晃动
  • 更宽的自由视角
  • 斗转星移：与视野中的任何目标瞬间互换位置
  • 功夫人机：随着你掌握每一门武功，人机也会学会并使用

🤖 画皮

  • 在普通战局中按阵营高亮人机身体以清晰识别：绿色为队友，红色为敌人，白色为中立
  • 在乐园死斗中用独特颜色高亮标记每支队伍
  • 可随时开启/关闭

🌌 新用户界面

  • 新主画面：重排按钮，新增2个新游戏模式按钮，移除“测试阶段警告”
  • 背景选择器：设置视频文件作为动态背景，或图片作为静态背景
  • 音乐播放器：设置音乐作为背景音乐，藏身处、局内也可播放
  • 江湖风格包

🎋 锋镝录

  • 每次命中后显示详细伤害统计：命中部位及伤害值，命中的护甲/头盔及其损耗的耐久度，以及被护甲/头盔吸收的伤害值
  • 每种人机类型的击杀记录：拾荒者、佣兵、头目等
  • 在混战死斗模式中：显示每支队伍的击杀/死亡比
  • 可自由移动并放置锋镝录在屏幕任何位置
  • 滚动查看战局历史，并自动保存副本至本地日志文件 BepInEx\plugins\JiangHu.Client\fight_log

这个模组最初起始于一个简单的想法：对原版任务去其糟粕、取其精华，将其重建为更专注、精炼的任务线，让我们获得更好的游戏体验。但在此过程中，由于单个任务的内容过多，可能会使其超出有趣或富有挑战性的范畴，反而变得令人沮丧。为了解决这个问题并使任务线更有趣，我们有了本次更新：

  • 最终任务现为“悬赏“任务：我们可以随时尝试此任务，无需任何前提条件，从1级开始。若成功，你将成为完成不可能之事的传奇，并可立即升级声望。

  • 从“塔科夫欢乐大家庭“任务中移除“不能使用止痛药“的要求；将“不能使用医疗物品“的要求从“生存专家“移至“绿洲 - 专3“。从游戏体验的角度看，限制机制可以让短线任务变得更好玩，但会让长线任务变得不合理。

  • 降低大多数击杀任务的数量要求，以及“塔科夫之家 - 第二部分“的藏身处等级要求，在保持挑战性的同时避免过度重复。

  • 更新了F12菜单中门户中心的信息
