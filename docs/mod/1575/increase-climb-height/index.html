Description Versions 6

Add verticality to your gameplay. Access previously inaccessible roofs. Hop fences you know you could hop irl.

This works much better than I ever thought it would. I have never gotten wedged or stuck outside the map anywhere. It works on all objects. Just look up a little and hold space.

Let me know if you find any good new spots. I have only played Woods.
