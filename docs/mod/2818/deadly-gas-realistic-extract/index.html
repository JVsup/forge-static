Deadly Gas — realistic end-of-raid

Replaces the arbitrary end-of-timer MIA with a deadly gas: when the raid timer expires, the zone slowly fills with gas (green haze + fog) and your health drains : about 3 minutes to die at full health (configurable). You can still run for the extract: extracting during the gas counts as a normal Survived. Dying in the gas is a normal death. No more MIA screen at 2 meters from the extract.

Gas masks allow you to stay 3 times longer in the gas. Respirators allow you to stay 1.5 times longer in the gas.

Client-side BepInEx plugin. Nothing to install server-side.

Install

Extract it into your SPT folder (the one with SPT.Launcher.exe). The DLL lands in BepInEx/plugins/MekkaDeadlyGas.dll.

Configuration

In game via F12 (Configuration Manager) or in BepInEx/config/com.mekka.deadlygas.cfg:

Setting Default Effect
Enabled on Master switch
Minutes before death 3 Survival time in the gas at full health (chest-based)
Gas rise (seconds) 20 Grace period before damage starts
Visual effects on Green tint + fog
Tint intensity 0.45 Green overlay opacity (0.2 subtle → 0.7 pea soup)
Fog density 0.06 Fog thickness at full ramp
Probe mode off Diagnostic dumps for troubleshooting

Known quirks

  • After expiry the in-raid timer display shows ~24h instead of 00:00 (cosmetic only, the gas is your real timer now).
  • AI Scavs are unaffected.
  • Works in PMC and player-Scav raids. Untested with Fika/coop.
