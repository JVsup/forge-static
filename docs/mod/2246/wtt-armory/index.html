WTT Armory

The WTT Armory series returns to SPT 4.0

The largest WTT standalone release from the WTT Team & Allies featuring:

  • 50+ weapons

  • A countless amount of attachments

  • Custom quests

  • Unique “Boss” weapon variants pushed to boss loadouts

  • Loose loot spawns hand placed around the world

  • 1,000+ combined man hours spread among 11 WTT developers and many WTT friends.

A Fresh Coat of Paint

Many of the familiar weapons you’ve seen from the armory series in the past have received a fresh coat of paint. In many cases, these weapons have been completely remastered with new models, textures, sounds, and even new attachments. Over time, our skills and knowledge have improved, and we’ve made sure to incorporate that into our weapons.

In addition to remastering old weapons, we’ve also added several new weapons and attachments to the official Armory roster.

World Immersion - Traders / Loot Spawns / Boss Loadouts

While WTT Armory weapons have always been available through traders, we’ve gone the extra lengths to develop a system to allow these weapons to spawn in the world in hand placed locations on each map. Can you find all of the locations? Let the scavenger hunt begin…

In addition to loose loot spawns, we’ve also created and pushed unique variants of weapons to boss loadouts. Now you’ll have a chance of encountering one of these rare unique weapons from defeating a boss.

Disclaimers & Other Information

The original Little Drummer Boy magazines have been incorporated into WTT Armory and can be found on the ragfair for a price…

New custom optics have been incorporated into WTT Armory and can be found on the ragfair.

The SPT 3.11 version of this mod requires Virtuals Custom Quest Loader for the custom quests to work properly!

What is WTT?

What is Welcome To THE CITY?

Welcome To THE CITY (WTT) is a mod for SPT AKI that has been in development for over a year. What started as a small passion project has grown in size to incorporate 15+ core members as well as contributions from other well-known mod authors and friends of WTT alike.

How does Welcome To THE CITY change the game?

As a mod, WTT overhauls SPT AKI, touching on almost every vanilla game system, as well as adding completely new systems to the game and its core mechanics. Inspired by open-world hardcore survival projects like STALKER: Anomaly and STALKER: GAMMA, WTT takes SPT’s artificial multiplayer MMO difficulty adders and replaces them with a challenging world economy, loot scarcity rebalancing, a lore friendly quest overhaul, and a map-traveling system built from rewritten portions of the Traveler mod allowing seamless travel between maps. Aside from its changes to the game’s core properties, WTT also adds to the world’s variety of traders, NPC’s, characters, items, and quests:

  • Over 1,000 custom bundles adding new weapons, attachments, wearables, containers, food, medical, and barter items.
  • New bosses with their own custom gear that will be properly incorporated into WTT’s quest lines.
  • New, meaningful traders with their own lore-friendly quests and narrative elements to help the player progress through the story-line of WTT.
  • New playable characters and outfits that will add to the player’s customization abilities.
  • New quest types incorporating brand new map extraction points, putting the player’s knowledge and familiarization of SPT’s maps to the test.

Welcome To THE CITY stands as one of the largest mods created for SPT AKI thus far.
