Quest Presence Detector

Detects when quest items are nearby by showing a progress bar with an optional arrow pointing towards it.

Description Versions 2

This mod lets you know when you are near a quest item by showing a progress bar that goes further the closer you are. An optional arrow can also be toggled (on by default) that points towards the objective. The size of the area where the mod triggers can be configured.
