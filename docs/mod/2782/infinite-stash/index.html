Infinite Stash

  • Virtually Unlimited Storage: Expands your stash to 10,000 rows (100,000 slots), giving you all the space you will ever need for your loot.
  • Safe Uninstallation: Before removing the mod, ensure all items are moved to the top. If even a single item remains in the expanded area, the extra slots will be deleted, but only down to the lowest remaining item in your stash.
