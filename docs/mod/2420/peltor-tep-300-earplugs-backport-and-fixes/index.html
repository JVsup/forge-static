TEP-300 Backport and Fixes

Backport of Peltor TEP-300 for SPTarkov 4.0.3 with fixes.
Included model has gray color and preserves all statistics of Peltor ComTac II headset from SPT.
Weight has been adjusted to be more realistic, instead just copying 1:1 existing headset, like Big Silly Goose did.

The earplugs are unlocked on Ragman LL2, no quests required.

For ones wondering why it’s 410g:

Total weight of TEP-300 with its case and batteries: 188g
https://multimedia.3m.com/mws/media/2208992O/3m-peltor-technical-data-specification-tep-300-usac.pdf
Remote Control Unit: 222g
https://multimedia.3m.com/mws/media/2208990O/3m-peltor-techn-data-spec-scu-300-usac.pdf

(Mod requires WTT-CommonLib version of at least 2.0.0)

How to install:

  1. Download .zip
  2. Unpack it
  3. Move SPT folder into your SPTarkov repository
