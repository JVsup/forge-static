CloudSix

This is a total replacement of THE CITY’s built in clouds.

Support my work on Ko-fi: https://ko-fi.com/matsix

After over analyzing my clouds in VR, I wrote a custom over engineered volumetric cloud shader that works in VR and flatscreen.

These clouds wouldn’t have been possible without the paper from Guerrilla Games on how they did the clouds in Horizon Zero Dawn - https://www.guerrilla-games.com/read/the-real-time-volumetric-cloudscapes-of-horizon-zero-dawn

My main focus with these clouds was keeping them visually stable and very optimized because I wanted them to work well in VR too. They could still use some improving, a lot of improving can be done just with the values I locked behind in advanced settings. You can also increase the primary steps/light steps higher by going into advanced. By default, this runs at half res, 64 primary steps on clouds and 6 light steps. I don’t believe there’s a big performance impact here unless you’re on older hardware.

Features:

  • Weather affects density
  • Wind affects cloud speed
  • Physically based volumetrics aware of sun/moon position
  • Procedural coloring based on THE CITY’s atmospherics
  • Well optimized - it performs well in VR also
  • Ground shadows based on cloud position

Update Changelog:

  • Improved cloud terrain shadow intensity, you should always get a shadow now when the sun is behind a cloud (the shadow intensity will be based on the density of the part of the cloud the sun is behind)
  • Fixed an issue where the shadows being casted by the clouds were reversed, i.e. if the sun was behind a cloud in front of you on the right of the screen, you would get a shadow on the left rather than on the right
  • Adjusted some shader values to improve the look of the clouds (reminder you can adjust pretty much everything I do if you enable the advanced bepinex settings)
  • Adjusted default wind values, clouds should move a bit faster now
  • Added settings to adjust the min and max wind speed. Max speed is when THE CITY’s wind is at its strongest, min obviously when it’s at its lowest. THE CITY’s wind changes dynamically. So, keep in mind, if you make the gap between these speeds too small/big you may have some strange side effects
  • Various performance optimizations to the shader that may or may not help to keep your GPU from melting

WARNING: Updates to this mod will always reset your config for it due to changes to the shader and other things requiring config values to be adjusted.
