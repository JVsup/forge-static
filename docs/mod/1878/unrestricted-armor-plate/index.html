Unrestricted Armor Plate

Allows to insert any Armor Plate into any Armored Chest Rig​ or Armor Vest​.

Description Versions 6

This is mainly for ArmorPlate, but also works on BuiltInInserts.
BuiltInInserts is disable by default. You can enable it in config.jsonc file.

Armor plates are divided into front+back, side.
BuiltInInserts are divided into front+back, side, groin, shoulder, collar.
BuiltInInserts for helmets are divided intro top, back, ears.

To get build-in you need take it from other armor/rig - there are no option to buy build-in.
PMC’s wills spawn with random available build-in, if BuiltInInserts are enabled.

Example preview
Example preview 2

Issues:

  • The quest Sew it Good - Part 2 is easier to complete if BuiltInInserts are enabled.
  • At least one armor (BNTI Zhuk body armor (Press)) uses armorPlateColliders from two systems at once (Plate_Granit_SAPI_chest, Plate_Korund_chest)

How to install:
Drop the contents of FiveF-unrestrictedArmorPlate-2.0.0.zip to the path of SPT.

Hot to uninstall:
Delete FiveF-unrestrictedArmorPlate folder from SPTarkov400\SPT\user\mods\.
