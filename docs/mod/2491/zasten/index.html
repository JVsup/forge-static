Installation Installation

  1. Open the downloaded 7z file in 7-zip

  2. Select the folder in the 7z file in 7-zip

  3. Drag the selected folder from 7-zip into your SPT folder

Zasten Mod – Patch Notes v2.2.2

Quest Fixes & Adjustments

Do It My Way – Part 3

  • Fixed progression issue where the P90 unlocked at Trader Loyalty Level 3 / PMC Level 27
  • P90 now unlocks at Trader Loyalty Level 2
  • Allows the weapon to be obtained and used as intended for quest completion
  • Updated EN locales for Do It My Way
    • Quest text now correctly states “Zasten’s build”
    • Clarifies that the weapon must be purchased directly from the trader
    • Removes ambiguity and prevents softlocks

New End-Game Quest

Chase the Rainbow

  • New high-difficulty end-game quest
  • Unlocks at PMC Level 37
  • Objective:
    • Find and hand over all colored Labs keycards
  • Designed as an extremely rare and challenging quest
  • Provides high-value rewards and exclusive unlocks

New Stim Added

SJ6-X “Blackout”

  • Unlocked after completing Chase the Rainbow
  • End-game combat stim with powerful short-term benefits and a severe crash phase

Buffs

  • +45% stamina regeneration for 180 seconds
  • +25 maximum stamina for 180 seconds
  • Quantum Tunnelling for 45 seconds
  • +1 health regeneration for 300 seconds
  • Removes all active blood loss effects for 180 seconds
  • +20 Vitality skill rate for 180 seconds

Post-Stim Crash Effects

  • Hands tremor after 45 seconds
  • -40% stamina regeneration
  • -50% hydration rate
  • -50% energy rate
  • -10 Metabolism skill rate for 300 seconds
  • -5 Health skill rate for 60 seconds

Summary

  • Fixed quest progression and early-game unlock issues
  • Improved clarity and reliability of quest requirements
  • Added new end-game quest with meaningful difficulty and rewards
  • Introduced a high-risk, high-reward exclusive stim
  • Improved overall quest stability and reward handling
