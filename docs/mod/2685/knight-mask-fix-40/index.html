FW Knight Mask Fix (SPT 4.0.13)

Fixes the missing “Death Knight” mask texture on Knight PMC and all player models.

Installation

  1. Drag or extract the ’SPT` folder inside of the downloaded “FWKnightMaskFix.zip” directly into your SPT root directory where EscapeFromTarkov.exe, server.exe, and launcher.exe are located.

Credits

Derivative of Umbigo Preto’s “Face the Knight - Mask Fix” (MIT License).
Thank you to Umbigo Preto for the original work that made this possible!

Compatibility

  • Tested on SPT 4.0.13+
  • Standalone (no dependencies)
  • Works with SAIN, SVM, and 99.9% of other mods.
