Installation

Decompress and put the folder SPT under [Your EFT Directory]/

Notice

  • Not completely compatible with profile created with AmightyTank’s Massivesoft Gun due to the inconsistent ID used by GRAU. Remove anything related with GRAU might work.

  • Tavor X95 & X95-R is not included since WTT have a better one.

  • To disable a certain component, remove the coresponding folder under MassivesoftWeapons/subs/

  • Theoretically compatible with Fika and WTT-armory. Not fully tested. No guarantee.

  • All models, textures, audio and animations ported from other games belongs to their respective original owner. No commercial use of this mod is allowed.
