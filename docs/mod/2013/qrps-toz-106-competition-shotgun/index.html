When in doubt, TOZ 'em out. A polymer-hydraulic stock and an upgraded main body, this TOZ is ready to rock Tarkov. An old classic by FancyDream, brought back from Aki 2.0 all the way to 3.10!

An upgraded TOZ, for when you want to style on PMCs even more.

The mod consists of the main weapon, the hydraulic stock, and the APFSDS rounds. The stock and rounds should also be able to be used on the original TOZ as well, if that’s more your jam.

FAQ:

Where buy? Jager LL2 for the gun, Jager LL3 for the APFSDS because they’re kinda cooked.

Realism? Yes

The APFSDS rounds aren’t realistic! Yes. But our boy the TOZ needs some love. Look at him, he’s so sad over there, neglected while all the AR15 attachments come out. He needs love, he needs APFSDS in his life.

Where original? Oddba

What QRPS? Qwerty-Reaper Porting Service (Turns out there’s a bunch of old bundles on Oddba that never got updated, and we decided to port some of them to give them one last hurrah before the Unity update bricks the bundles, also I like acronyms.)

Credits to the original author FancyDream! We wouldn’t have this now if you didn’t originally make these awesome bundles!
