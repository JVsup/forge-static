ABOUT THE MOD

SALCO’s ARMORY is a modular equipment and gameplay expansion for SPT, adding both real-world and fictional items.

Features

  • Improved compatibility between weapons, attachments, and supported mods
  • Combining of partially used medical items
  • Configurable gameplay systems
  • Compatibility with popular bot mods such as SAIN, ABPS, and ORBIT
  • A merchant named “Wayland”.

Countermeasure Protocol

The Countermeasure Protocol analyzes your recent PMC raids and identifies recurring tactics. Depending on your playstyle, a limited number of enemy PMCs may receive suitable equipment to counter night raids, headshots, long-range combat, suppressed weapons, or heavy armor.

SALCO’s ARMORY does not control AI behavior or bot spawning. Its systems can be configured or disabled to suit your preferred experience.

INSTALLATION
  1. Backup your profile before every update!!!
  2. Delete every previous version completely!!!
  3. Clear your caches!!!
  4. Download the mod!!!
  5. Extract the ZIP!!!
  6. Copy the contents of the ZIP into your SPT root folder!!!

This mod is NOT compatible with SALCO’s ARMORY (the old version), SALCO’s ARSENAL, COMBAT ARSENAL and COMBAT ARSENAL: Legacy.

Only use this mod if:

  • you are starting a completely new save file.
  • you have deleted the old mod and all its items from your profile.
  • Do not mix versions.
UNINSTALLATION
  1. Delete all items from the mod from your profile. You can recognise them by the fact that they begin with “SALCO’s ARMORY:”. Then close the game and the server.
  2. Locate the file core.json in ...SPT\SPT_Data\configs\ and open it with a text editor of your choice.
  3. Once you have opened the file, find the entry "removeModItemsFromProfile": false, and set the value to true. Then save the file.
  4. Start the server and wait until the server has deleted all files that are part of the mod.
  5. You can now close the server and change the value back from true to false.

FAQ

Q: Is this mod compatible with other versions such as SALCO’s ARSENAL, COMBAT ARSENAL or COMBAT ARSENAL: Legacy?
A: Technically speaking, yes. However, I strongly advise you NOT to use these mods together. Choose one version. Support is only available for this one, though.

Q: Could you please add item XY to the game?
A: No! I design the mod the way I want to. Adding new content to the game takes time and, in some cases, money as well. Please bear this in mind.

Q: Why do you keep deleting the mods and uploading them under a new name?
A: I can see why it might look that way, and to be honest, I’m sorry that it gives that impression, as there’s not much I can do about it. But please understand that I won’t be commenting on this any further.

Q: Will there be new assets in the future?
A: Yes! Handguards, buttstocks, foregrips and so on will be making a comeback in the mod. But I still need a bit more time.

Q: Is SALCO’s ARMORY related to WTT?
A: Not a single bit.

New Feature
Countermeasure Protocol (CP) is a new adaptive gameplay system that learns from your recent PMC raids and allows enemy PMCs to prepare countermeasures against your preferred tactics. For example, frequent night raids may lead to more night-vision equipment, accurate headshots may result in additional face protection, and repeated long-range engagements may cause enemies to equip suitable optics. Other possible responses include improved hearing protection and more effective ammunition. The system begins analyzing your performance after three completed PMC raids. Only 25–35% of eligible PMC bots receive countermeasures, with a maximum of two per bot. This keeps encounters dynamic without turning every enemy into a perfectly equipped counter to the player. Countermeasure Protocol only adjusts completed bot loadouts. It does not control spawning, movement, combat behavior, personalities, looting, or extraction logic, helping it remain compatible with popular bot mods such as SAIN, ABPS, and ORBIT. It is fully configurable unter countermeasure_protocol.jsonc.
