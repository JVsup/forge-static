Nemesis Protocol

Nemesis Protocol turns ordinary PMC encounters into a persistent rivalry that develops across multiple raids.

Instead of every enemy PMC being a disposable, randomly generated opponent, the mod creates a recurring rival who can remember previous encounters, grow stronger, improve their equipment, and return in future raids.

Your nemesis is generated as a normal USEC or BEAR PMC, allowing other AI mods to continue controlling their combat behavior, navigation, objectives, looting, and extraction logic. Nemesis Protocol focuses on the persistent identity and progression behind that PMC rather than replacing the existing AI system.

Every encounter contributes to an ongoing rivalry. Kill your nemesis and they may return stronger. Let them escape and they can continue progressing. If they kill you, their threat level increases and the rivalry becomes more dangerous.

Key Features

Persistent Rival PMC

Nemesis Protocol creates a recurring enemy PMC with a persistent identity that carries across raids.

The mod stores information such as:

  • Name and faction
  • Current level and rank
  • Encounter history
  • Player kills and nemesis deaths
  • Successful escapes
  • Rivalry progression
  • Last generated equipment tier
  • Previous raid outcomes

Your nemesis is not regenerated from scratch after every raid. Their history follows them.

Dynamic Progression

The nemesis can gain levels based on configurable events, including:

  • Killing the player
  • Surviving and escaping a raid
  • Reappearing in later raids
  • Reaching new rivalry milestones

Level growth, health scaling, appearance frequency, and progression limits can all be adjusted through the included configuration file.

NEMESIS NETWORK

Nemesis Network is a separate Messenger contact that functions as the player’s rivalry database and command interface.

Available commands:

  • help
  • status
  • rivals
  • history
  • rival 1
  • rival
  • compat
  • compatibility

Nemesis Network can be used to:

  • View the active Nemesis
  • Review previous rivals
  • Inspect encounter statistics
  • Check rivalry progression
  • Search for a rival by name
  • View APBS compatibility status
  • View ORBIT compatibility status

Nemesis Network handles records and commands, while individual rivals handle their personal messages and taunts.

APBS/ORBIT Dependencies

If you don’t want to use APBS or ORBIT to control the Nemesis behavior/equipment, simply change the following in your config.jsonc:

“UseApbsProgression”: false, “PreserveOrbitPmcRole”: false, “ExactLevelFallbackWithoutApbs”: false,

APBS Progression Support

When Acid’s Progressive Bot System — APBS is installed, Nemesis Protocol passes the nemesis progression level into APBS during bot generation.

APBS remains responsible for generating the final:

  • PMC level
  • Equipment tier
  • Weapons
  • Armor
  • Ammunition
  • Inventory
  • Loot

This allows the nemesis to naturally receive stronger and more appropriate equipment as the rivalry develops.

Nemesis Protocol does not overwrite the final APBS-generated loadout after creation.

ORBIT Compatibility

The nemesis remains a standard USEC or BEAR PMC instead of being converted into a custom boss or unsupported bot role.

This allows ORBIT to continue managing the PMC’s normal:

  • Objectives
  • Navigation
  • Combat behavior
  • Looting
  • Decision-making
  • Extraction behavior

Nemesis Protocol manages the persistent rival identity while ORBIT manages what that PMC does during the raid.

Configurable Spawn System

Control how often your nemesis appears and where encounters can occur.

Configuration options include:

  • Base spawn chance
  • Minimum raids between encounters
  • Maximum progression level
  • Disabled locations
  • PMC-side requirements
  • Progression rewards
  • Health scaling
  • Rival messages
  • APBS integration
  • ORBIT compatibility behavior

Every option is documented directly inside the included config.jsonc file.

Persistent Encounter History

Nemesis Protocol records the developing rivalry between the player and the recurring PMC.

Depending on the outcome of each raid, the mod can track whether:

  • The player killed the nemesis
  • The nemesis killed the player
  • The nemesis survived
  • The nemesis escaped
  • The encounter ended without a confirmed result

This history remains connected to the player profile and can continue through future raids.

Rival Messages and Status Commands

The nemesis can communicate through the SPT messaging system, providing updates and rivalry-themed responses.

Available chat commands allow the player to inspect information such as:

  • Current nemesis status
  • Rivalry progression
  • Encounter history
  • APBS detection
  • ORBIT detection
  • Current compatibility state

Message pools can be customized in the configuration.

Profile Migration

Older Nemesis Protocol rivalry profiles are automatically migrated when newer fields are introduced.

This allows players to update the mod without intentionally resetting their existing nemesis history.

Compatibility

Designed for:

  • SPT 4.0.13
  • APBS
  • ORBIT
  • Standard USEC and BEAR PMC generation

Nemesis Protocol is a server-side mod and installs under:

user/mods/ForgeHQ-NemesisProtocol

A separate Nemesis BepInEx plugin is not required for the current feature set.

Installation

  1. Download and extract the archive.
  2. Merge the included folder with your main SPT installation.
  3. Confirm the mod is located at:

SPT/user/mods/ForgeHQ-NemesisProtocol

  1. Start the SPT server.
  2. Review config.jsonc to customize the experience.

Updating

When installing an update, preserve the following folder to retain existing rivalry data:

user/mods/ForgeHQ-NemesisProtocol/data/profiles

Do not replace or delete that folder unless you intentionally want to reset your nemesis.

Configuration

The included config.jsonc contains readable descriptions above every setting.

Users can configure:

  • Nemesis appearance frequency
  • Level gains
  • Health growth
  • Maximum level
  • Disabled maps
  • Message text
  • APBS progression handling
  • ORBIT compatibility
  • Logging and debugging options

Restart the SPT server after changing the configuration.

Important Notes

Nemesis Protocol controls the persistent identity, history, progression, and spawn scheduling of the rival PMC.

It does not replace the full PMC AI system. Combat behavior remains controlled by the player’s existing AI setup, including ORBIT, SAIN, BigBrain, Waypoints, or standard SPT behavior where applicable.

The result is a recurring enemy who still behaves like part of the normal SPT ecosystem—but has a reason to remember you.

Your raids are no longer isolated encounters. Someone is coming back.
