Description Versions 4

The scavs in the THE CITY area have recently bought a 3d printer off of fence and have been going mad with thingiverse models of Halo helmets. They’re out to make a quick rouble and look sleek doing it.

Ragman has agreed to sell their wares for them, along with some surplus gear from the Cold War and older. Did it come from a museum? It certainly belongs in one

Thanks to EpicRangeTime for the shaders!
