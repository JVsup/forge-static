Description

Overview

The kit I use to test my own mods without the game getting in the way: build the situation you need, hold it still, and read exactly what happened. Everything is off by default and lives in F12.

It isn’t a passive overlay. It spawns, freezes and kills bots, steers your own shots and hands you items — a workbench, not something to take into a raid you care about.

Staying alive while you work

  • Invincible — no health or armor damage.
  • Weapon Godmode — the held mag stays topped up, no durability loss, no malfunctions.
  • Movement Godmode — infinite stamina, no carry-weight penalty.

Building the situation

  • Spawn any bot at your crosshair — pick the type, difficulty and count. The type list is read from the running game, so modded factions are in it without the mod knowing they exist. Bots come through the game’s own spawn pipeline, so they get real loadouts, brains and grouping, and they land on walkable ground where you’re looking.
  • Spawn any item — a searchable catalogue of everything the client knows about, modded items included, filtered by category. Click to have it placed in your inventory, marked found-in-raid.
  • Freeze Bots holds them where they stand, Bots Ignore Me drops you off their target list, and Kill All Bots clears the board between tests.

Reading the result

  • Limb Aim Assist steers your shots onto a chosen body part, so your aim isn’t part of the experiment. Only the direction changes — real ballistics, so armor, penetration and pellet spread all behave.
  • Hit Log prints what each hit did: the damage that reached flesh, whether the round beat the armor or was stopped, and the armor damage left behind.
  • Bot Loadout Inspector shows a bot’s whole generated kit — weapons with fitted mods, armor, stacks and condition — so you can check a loadout config without killing and looting the body.
  • Performance Overlay — frames per second, frame time and the live bot count.

Seeing through walls

  • ESP draws bots in your choice of Box, Skeleton, Chams — a whole-silhouette through-wall highlight tinted per limb by HP — or plain Labels, with name, HP and range.

  • Custom-faction bots read as their actual faction — RUAF, UNTAR, Black Division, Wedge and the rest, with a faction’s escort tagged GUARD — resolved live from MoreBotsAPI when it’s installed, with a baked name table behind it.

  • Through a magnified optic the box/skeleton/label overlays bow out: scopes render through a camera of their own, so screen-projected shapes can’t line up inside the glass. Chams still work zoomed in, and red dots and irons keep the full overlay.

Getting around, and the world

  • Freecam flies you anywhere, through anything, and puts you back where you started. Waypoints save a spot and jump you back to it later.
  • Time Of Day moves the raid clock and the sun with it, and Weather gives you fog, rain, cloud and wind — both for optics, night vision and glare work.
Installation

Requirements

Nothing. One optional integration, detected at runtime and ignored if absent:

Mod GUID What you lose without it
MoreBotsAPI com.morebotsapi.tacticaltoaster Live faction names on the ESP. Custom bots still label off the known ID ranges.

Steps

  1. Download the archive.
  2. Extract it into your SPT folder — the one holding EscapeFromTarkov.exe and BepInEx.
  3. Merge when Windows asks. DevTools lands in BepInEx/plugins.
  4. Launch. Everything is off until you flip it in F12.

Client-side only. Nothing goes in user/mods.

Configuration

In-game F12, or edit BepInEx/config/com.sipto.devtools.cfg.

Around fifty settings across eleven sections. The ones worth knowing before you start:

Immunity Invincible / Weapon Godmode / Movement Godmode off Damage, ammo and durability, stamina and weight
ESP Enable ESP off Style: Box / Skeleton / Chams / Labels. Max Distance 0 is no limit
Aim Limb Aim Assist off Target Limb, Aim FOV, and whether it steers every shot or only ADS

Compatibility

Client-side only — no server mod, nothing in user/mods, and nothing networked.

It does drive the raid when you ask it to: it spawns, freezes and kills bots, steers your own shots and hands you items. Bots belong to the host, so on a co-op server those bot controls act for everyone in the raid, not just you. It’s a testing tool — keep it out of raids you care about, and out of co-op raids other people care about.

In a Fika group only you need it installed.

Built against SPT 4.0.13.

Dev Tools 1.8.0

1.0.0 was just godmode and an ESP. This turns it into a full test bench: spawn what you want, freeze it, shoot it where you choose, and read exactly what happened.

Everything is off by default, client-side, and lives under F12. Nothing is networked — no server mod, no packets — but it does drive the raid when you ask it to, and bots belong to the host, so on a co-op server bot spawning, freezing and killing act for everyone in the raid.

Shooting and damage

  • Limb Aim Assist — steer your shots onto a chosen body part (head, thorax, stomach, either arm or leg) of the bot nearest your crosshair. Only the shot direction changes; the round keeps its real ballistics, so armor zones, penetration and pellet spread all behave. Your view never moves. An Aim FOV cone decides what counts as the target, and Trigger picks between steering every shot or only shots fired while aiming down sights.
  • Hit Log — what each hit actually did: body part, the damage that reached flesh, whether the round beat the armor (with its penetration value) or was stopped, plus armor damage, target and range. Penetrating and blocked hits are coloured differently.

Spawning

  • Spawn any bot at your crosshair — pick a type, difficulty and count, press a key. The type list is read from the running game, so modded factions appear automatically without this mod knowing they exist. Bots come through the game’s own spawn pipeline, so they get real loadouts, brains and grouping, and they are placed on walkable ground at your aim point.
  • Spawn any item — a searchable catalogue of every item the client knows about, modded items included. Filter by category, click to have it placed in your inventory.

Bots

  • Bots Ignore Me — drop off every bot’s target list, including bots already hunting you. They carry on fighting each other.
  • Freeze Bots — they stop thinking, moving and shooting where they stand. Toggling off resumes them; nothing is reset.
  • Kill All Bots — clear the map between tests.
  • Bot Loadout Inspector — aim at a bot and read its whole generated kit: weapons with their fitted mods, armor, rig, helmet, stacks, and condition. For checking a loadout config came out the way you wrote it, without killing the bot and looting the body. Positionable, so it need not sit on your minimap.

Getting around and seeing

  • Freecam — fly anywhere, through anything. It flies you rather than a detached camera, so the view, mouse look and HUD behave normally; toggling off returns you to where you took off from.
  • Waypoints — save a spot and teleport back to it later. Survives a restart.
  • ESP gains a text-only Labels style and a Show Distance toggle.

World

  • Time Of Day moves the raid clock and the sun with it, for optics, night vision and glare work.
  • Weather — fog, rain, cloud and wind, using the game’s own override, so switching it off restores whatever the raid rolled.
  • Performance Overlay — frames per second, frame time and the live bot count.

Notes

Everything is a testing aid and defaults to off; nothing changes unless you turn it on. Keep it out of raids you care about, and out of co-op raids other people care about.
