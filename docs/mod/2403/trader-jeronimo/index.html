Description Versions 3

Simple trader mod that sells ammo boxes (meta ammo) and used weapon presets. The selling price is the handbook value minus 5%.

Buys the following items at a price close to other traders:

  • Weapons
  • Ammo
  • Ammo Box
  • Magazine
  • Weapon Mods
  • Assault Scope
  • Optic Scope
  • Special Scope
  • Compact Collimator
  • Collimator

Weapon Presets

Weapons with 70 durability:

  • Custom AKS-74UN
  • Custom M4A1

Weapons with 80 durability:

  • Colt M4A1 5.56x45 assault rifle USASOC-I
  • Kalashnikov AK-74N 5.45x39 assault rifle Magpul

Weapons with 90 durability:

  • Colt M4A1 5.56x45 assault rifle T.O 2K24
  • SIG MCX .300 Blackout assault rifle T.O 2K24

Weapon with 100 durability:

  • TheAkGuy AK-50

Suggested mods: AmmoBox1x1 TraderScrolling

Ammo Tier List from https://www.eft-ammo.com/

New weapon presets will be added in the future

For modders, if you want to use this trader to sell your items, the traderID is 68f581fb978611acbb3d5094. Your PostDBModLoader must be at least +3.

📁Installation

  • Drag folder from zip file directly into your SPT install folder
