Getting started with quests

  • Accept Whispers Through Shattered Glass from Therapist to unlock Euphoria
  • Follow a straight chain with supply runs, reliability checks, quiet deliveries and rewards
  • After Bad Habit, pick up Vapor Substitute from Mechanic
  • Rewards are various barter items, bullets, mags, guns, etc..
Trader

Anya “Euphoria” Zaytseva

Anya grew up around noise and cheap tools. If something closed right and held, she trusted it. When Norvinsk locked down, she stayed. She watched what people carried, what failed, and what they were willing to pay for when things got rough.

She took over a small signal shack by the rail, painted the door black, and started calling it the Dragon Den. Inside is simple and tidy. Pegboard with straps and buckles. Racks for rifles that actually run. Backpacks stacked by fit, not fashion. A small case for valuables that do not scream their price. Then there is the shelf she pretends is just barter. Cat paw zipper pulls, bright sling keepers, small charms, sticker sheets. She rolls her eyes when you point at it, then restocks it anyway.

She is new to contracting. No speeches, no sob stories. You say what you need, you pay clean, you leave on time. She deals in equipment that works, weapons that cycle, packs that do not quit, valuables that travel quiet, and the odd cute thing that makes a bad day lighter. She does not patch wounds and she does not promise miracles.
Therapist is the one person she will change plans for. They argue, then they sort it, and neither one wastes words. People come to Euphoria for straight deals and leave with gear that fits and a pack that will not betray them. At closing she lines up the next day’s crates, writes names on tape, and locks the door. If you ask why she keeps at it, she says, “Good gear should be easy to buy,” and gets back to work.

What is Dragon Den

Dragon Den is my twist on Tarkov. It is where cuteness, edgyness, and weirdness collide while staying believable.

  • Traders with their own quirks and attitude
  • Custom items and quests that feel fresh
  • Backpacks, armor, facewear and headwear with personality
  • Odd creations that should not exist but still fit right in

No weapons, just everything else that makes Tarkov less boring.
