Walther WA-2000 Realism and Ammo update
Changes Overview
This document details all changes made to the Walther WA-2000 sniper rifle and its
ammunition in the mod. The updates include adjustments to the weapon’s base stats,
detailed specifications for four .300 Winchester Magnum ammo types (FMJ, HP, Tracer,
and AP), and a range of attachments with their effects. These changes ensure realistic
performance for a precision bullpup sniper rifle, optimized for a skilled marksman capable
of long-range accuracy and control. A short history of the WA-2000 and its ammo types
is included, along with reasoning for each modification to enhance gameplay and realism.

History of the Walther WA-2000
The Walther WA-2000, introduced in the 1980s by the German manufacturer Walther,
is a semi-automatic bullpup sniper rifle designed for law enforcement and military precision shooting. Chambered primarily in .300 Winchester Magnum, it was developed in response to a demand for a compact, highly accurate rifle following the 1972 Munich
Olympics attack, where maneuverability and precision were critical. Its bullpup configuration placing the action behind the trigger allowed

for a long barrel in a shorter overall package, making it unique among sniper rifles of its time. Only 176 units were produced due to its high cost and complexity, making it a rare and iconic firearm. In the game, the WA-2000 reflects this legacy as a top-tier sniper rifle,

blending historical authenticity with practical customization. We are going to pretend that Walther managed to sell a major contract and that they are relatively common among counter terrorism, SF units, and Private Operators. (There is a clone being made here in the US, but I haven’t seen recent updates.)

Changes to the WA-2000
The WA-2000’s base stats have been tuned to reflect its real-world precision and bullpup
design, optimized for a skilled sniper. Below are the changes and their reasoning:
• Weight: Set to 4.5 kg (base, adjusted for attachments).
– Reasoning: The real WA-2000 weighs approximately 6.95 kg, but the in-game weight
is reduced for balance, reflecting a stripped-down version suitable for agile gameplay
while retaining its hefty feel. Attachments adjust this further as needed.
• Ergonomics: Set to 42.
– Reasoning: The bullpup layout improves handling over traditional rifles, and this
value reflects a skilled user’s ability to wield it effectively, balancing realism with
gameplay fluidity.
• Recoil: RecoilForceUp: 450, RecoilForceBack: 900.
– Reasoning: The .300 Winchester Magnum produces significant recoil, but these
values are manageable for a trained marksman, allowing for quick follow-up shotskey
for a semi-automatic sniper rifle in dynamic engagements.
• Accuracy: CenterOfImpact: 0.01 (approximately 1 MOA).
– Reasoning: The WA-2000 is renowned for sub-MOA accuracy in real life. This stat
ensures pinpoint precision, rewarding skilled aim at long ranges, true to its historical
design.
• Fire Rate: bFirerate: 120 (2-3 rounds per second).
– Reasoning: As a semi-automatic rifle, the WA-2000 allows rapid shots compared
to bolt-action counterparts. This rate reflects a skilled user’s trigger discipline,
enhancing its combat versatility.
• Iron Sight Range: Set to 100 meters.
– Reasoning: While typically used with optics, the 100-meter iron sight range suits
close-to-mid-range engagements, with scopes extending its reachaligned with its
real-world adaptability.

Changes to .300 Winchester Magnum Ammo
The WA-2000 uses four .300 Winchester Magnum ammo types, each based on real-world
counterparts. Below are their details, histories, applications, and reasoning for changes.

.300 Winchester Magnum Sierra MatchKing 190gr FMJ
• History: Introduced by Sierra Bullets in the 1950s, the MatchKing line became a
gold standard for precision shooters. The 190-grain .300 Winchester Magnum variant
excels in long-range competitions due to its high ballistic coefficient.
• Real-World Applications: Used in sniper training and competitive shooting, prized
for accuracy over penetration, especially in military and civilian marksmanship events. Can’t forget, Chris Kyle’s preferred cartridge!

• Stats:
– Damage: 88
– Penetration: 45
– Bullet Velocity: 880 m/s
– Fragmentation Chance: 20%
– Ricochet Chance: 48%
– Armor Damage: 48%
• Reasoning: Stats emphasize precision and moderate penetration, mirroring its realworld role as a match-grade round. High velocity and accuracy make it ideal for
long-range headshots against armored foes, balanced by lower damage for gameplay
fairness.
• Flea Price: 1155

.300 Winchester Magnum InterLock HP
• History: Hornadys InterLock design, launched in the 1970s, revolutionized hollow
point bullets by locking the core and jacket together, ensuring reliable expansion. The
180-grain .300 Win Mag version is a hunting staple.
• Real-World Applications: Popular for big game hunting (e.g., elk, bear), where its
expansion delivers devastating wounds while minimizing over-penetration, also used
in law enforcement for its stopping power. (I once field cleaned a buck with a 300 win mag interlok…yeah its nasty)

• Stats:
– Damage: 95
– Penetration: 28
– Bullet Velocity: 899 m/s
– Fragmentation Chance: 70%
– Heavy Bleeding Chance: 38%
– Armor Damage: 48%
• Reasoning: High damage and fragmentation reflect its hunting roots, excelling against
unarmored targets. Lower penetration balances its lethality, making it a situational
choice for flesh damage over armor piercing.
• Flea Price: 845

.300 Winchester Magnum A191 Tracer
• History: Tracer rounds date back to World War I, with the A191 variant developed
for modern military use. The .300 Win Mag tracer aids snipers in tracking shots,
especially in low-light conditions.
• Real-World Applications: Employed in military training and combat to adjust
aim at long ranges, its glowing path enhances situational awareness for snipers and
spotters.

• Stats:
– Damage: 75
– Penetration: 58
– Bullet Velocity: 890 m/s
– Fragmentation Chance: 14%
– Armor Damage: 68%
• Reasoning: Balanced stats with high penetration and armor damage reflect its military utility, while lower damage ensures its not overpowered. The tracer effect aids
visibility, adding tactical depth.
• Flea Price: 1200

.300 Winchester Magnum Swiss P AP
• History: Swiss P armor-piercing rounds, featuring tungsten carbide cores, emerged
in the late 20th century to counter light armor. The 196-grain .300 Win Mag version
is a specialized anti-material round.
• Real-World Applications: Used by military and law enforcement to penetrate vehicle armor, barriers, or fortified positions, offering precision with destructive capability.
• Stats:
– Damage: 78
– Penetration: 60
– Bullet Velocity: 855 m/s
– Fragmentation Chance: 13%
– Armor Damage: 68%
• Reasoning: Top-tier penetration and armor damage mirror its real-world anti-armor
role, with moderate damage keeping it balanced. Slightly lower velocity reflects the
heavier core, adding realism.
• Flea Price: 1500

Attachments
The WA-2000 supports a variety of attachments, enhancing its versatility. Below are the
additions and their reasoning:
• Magazines: WA2000_MAG (6-round), WA2000_MAG10 (10-round), WA2000_MAG15
(15-round).
– Reasoning: The real WA-2000 used a 6-round magazine, but larger options (10
and 15) are added for gameplay flexibility, reflecting aftermarket possibilities while
keeping the base authentic.
• Bipod: WA2000_BIPOD (reduces recoil when deployed).
– Reasoning: A common sniper accessory, it stabilizes shots at long range, aligning
with the WA-2000s precision focus.
• Handguards: WA2000_HANDGUARD (standard), WA2000_SPECHANDGUARD
(tactical with rails).
– Reasoning: The tactical version adds modularity for modern attachments, enhancing customization while the standard retains historical accuracy.
• Barrels: WA2000_BARREL (standard), WA2000_HVYBARREL (increases accuracy), WA2000_EXTBARREL (boosts velocity), WA2000_INTGRLBARREL (balanced).
– Reasoning: Variants allow players to tweak performance for precision, velocity for range, or balance; mimicking real-world barrel options.
• Muzzles: WA2000_MUZZLE (muzzle brake, reduces recoil).
– Reasoning: Reduces the .300 Win Mags kick, improving control for rapid shots, a
practical upgrade for a semi-auto sniper.
• Grips: WA2000_GRIP (standard), WA2000_TACGRIP (improves ergonomics), WA2000_LIGHTG(reduces weight).
– Reasoning: Options cater to handling preferences, reflecting real customization
trends for comfort or agility.
• Stocks: WA2000_PAD (standard), WA2000_SOFTPAD (reduces recoil).
– Reasoning: The soft pad mitigates recoil further, enhancing comfort for sustained
use, a plausible upgrade.
• Scopes: S&B_1X8 (1-8x), S&B_3X12 (3-12x), S&B_5X25 (5-25x).
– Reasoning: Variable magnification suits all ranges, from close-quarters to extreme
distances, matching the WA-2000s sniper role.
