Have you ever wanted to put lead down range, but you want a somewhat wholesome twist? Well, introducing this dumb retexture mod that Hot Dog helped create. This will be part of a bigger pack that me and him will be authoring called “The Bluey Pack” with firearms and even a tactical vest that has a morale patch on it. Don’t expect there to be a Bluey themed everything, this is for strictly AR/AK and their deriatives only, mainly because of simplicity. We will eventually get around to doing Bluey themed pistols, but that will come in a part 2 to the original modpack.

Installation instructions: Drag the “Oi Cunt” folder into your user/mods structured folder. DO NOT DRAG THE ZIP. Drag the folder that’s inside it into your user/mods and it should work.

And yes, before you ask, yes I know the magazine does not match the gun’s color. That’s intentional, as it’s an imported magazine from Australia.

Expect more from us, and please do let us know if there are any bugs. I am not an experienced modder yet. Credits go to HotDog for helping me with the texture portion, and him for doing the coding side of it. I did the texturing portion, and he did the code.

Hopefully I will wind up making more mods for this game, as I love the community in the WTT server. They’re friendly as fuck and are able to answer a lot of my dumb questions without getting angry or frustrated with me.

Anyway, enjoy this mod!
