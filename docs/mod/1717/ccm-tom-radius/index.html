Tom Radius

A Character to be used.

in extension with

CCM ( Combined Configurations Mod )

serves as the template for all Characters to be based on.

giving them skills and attributes within those parameters.

made possible by

SVM - and the CCM Preset

they are ment to be used in

COOP.

with SPT-Realism and Path To Tarkov

by Fontaine and Trap.

however, it is not a requirement.

they can also be used and enjoyed, for vanilla SPT.

with whatever individual MOD combination

you use.

For further information about

CCM

Tom Radius, the younger of the two Norvinsk brothers, is bound not by blood but by combat. USEC and BEAR have united into a two-man army, capable of handling any scenario an active combat zone might bring.

USEC-trained American: Tom is agile and specializes in close-quarters combat, excelling with SMGs and other small-caliber platforms. On the other hand, there’s the brute strength of BEAR’s Ukranian: Sergiy, delivering sheer firepower with LMGs and relying on core body strength.

Together, as polar opposites, they form a perfect unity, a diverse combination of capabilities, battle-proven with a high body count. They are feared in Norvinsk.

Skills:

  • Agility
  • Small MachineGun Training
  • Endurance

Weaknesses:

  • Long range

  • Strength

  • Direct hit on any body part

    Installation. 1 and 2.** Make sure you have both Borkel´s and Kopa1ch´s clothing packs
    Link at the bottom

    installation 1, for only Tom only. with no backup system. or extension for future CCM Characters to be stored
    1. Download zip file.
    2. Extract user folder inside Folder “installation 1” into root SPT
    3. Done.

    installation 2, + CCM extension for Future CCM characters to automaticly be stored in
    same concept. 1. Download zip file
    2 extract user inside folder “instalaltion 2” into root SPT
    3. navigate to user/ profiles - CCM Characters folder
    4. Backup all your existing profile.json files.
    5. go to CCM, and copy Tom ( or whatever CCM Character you wish to play ) into profiles folder.
    6. Done

    The Clothing used by Tom is made by authors.
