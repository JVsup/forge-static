🇨🇦 True North 🇨🇦

Welcome to the True North

Juicy Trudy from Canmore has gotten a hold of some fancy contraband from his last fishing trip and he’s trying to offload it on the cheap.
He’s also recently purchased a Dremel from Canadian Tire and has a few new parts for you to try out.

To unlock the trader, you must complete the quest “Oh, Canada?” from Peacekeeper.

huge shoutout to EpicRangeTime for making the shaders that I use in this mod, go check out his work as well.

Installation

To install, unzip and drop the SPT folder into your SPT install location.
