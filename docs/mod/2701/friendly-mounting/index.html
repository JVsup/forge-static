Friendly Mounting

What does it do ?:

Allows you to mount your weapon on your team mates for some tactical sniping.

Automatically detaches if one of you moves or if your mate rotates too much.

Shooting an LMG next to your friends head might induce some dizziness.

Installation:

  1. Open the downloaded zip file.

  2. Drag the BepInEx folder from the zip file into your SPT folder. Needs to be installed on the headless.

Disclaimer:
