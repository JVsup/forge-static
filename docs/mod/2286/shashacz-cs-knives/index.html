GOLD! GOLD! GOLD! NOW IN THE CITY!

Got addicted to SPT mod making cause of my RX-01 project and started working on this little ambitious project where I am trying to port every knife from Counter Strike 2 (R.I.P CS:GO).

I know the skins are not perfect and I tried my best, it’s a pain in the ass making every texture by hand because counter strike uses patterns.

DROP RATE

- Knives are FIR only.

- They are separated into 3 tiers and each tier has a different price range and drop rate from in raid containers.

- PLEASE READ BELOW

SPT LEADERBOARD COLLABORATION

- Me and the devs at SPT Leaderboard teamed up and added our own cases on their website! So now you can earn currency on their website through pure skill and then gamble your life away to build your in game knife collection! keep in mind the currency is earned for free, there are no microtransactions

CURRENTLY ADDED KNIVES (6/20)

- Bayonet

- Bowie

- Butterfly knife

- Huntsman knife

- Karambit

- Shadow Dagger

SHOWCASE

- Little showcase can be found here.

INSTALLATION

- Drag and drop the user folder into your SPT root folder
