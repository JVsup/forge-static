LORE

Once a merchant navigating the bustling markets of Istanbul, Yilmaz ventured into more dangerous trades as the world’s black market began to flourish. Initially trading in rare gems and antique weapons, Yilmaz’s reputation grew for his ability to acquire anything for the right price. His keen sense of risk and opportunity led him to THE CITY, a city in chaos, where borders and laws meant little.

With THE CITY’s brutal civil conflict intensifying, Yilmaz found his true calling in the arms trade. A master smuggler, he began to supply weapons and equipment to both sides of the conflict, ensuring a steady flow of goods into the city. Known for his network of underground contacts that spanned across Europe and the Middle East, Yilmaz became a trusted yet elusive figure to mercenaries, PMCs, and desperate survivors alike. His ability to smuggle high-grade military weapons, forbidden tech, and rare goods in and out of THE CITY made him both feared and respected.

Yilmaz is driven by one goal: profit. Yet, despite his cutthroat nature, he holds onto a strict set of principles inherited from his time as a legitimate trader. Deals with Yilmaz are fair—so long as you have something of value to trade. Betray him, however, and you’ll be hunted down by his loyal band of mercenaries.

Hidden away in the shadows of THE CITY, Yilmaz is an essential contact for those looking to gain an edge in the city’s deadly warzone, dealing in everything from Kalashnikovs to rare ammunition, even military-grade explosives—if you can meet his price. His allegiance is to the market, and to himself.

Rumor has it that Yilmaz has plans beyond THE CITY’s borders. Those who know him well whisper about his desire to create a syndicate that could control arms trading across Eastern Europe, leveraging the chaos in THE CITY as a stepping stone for even greater power. For now, though, Yilmaz remains in the shadows, waiting for the right moment to capitalize on THE CITY’s anarchy.
