VR Gloves

Visually get rid of your old body that doesn't match your movements, mask your vision and cause motion sickness.

Description Versions 1

The VR mod from Cybensis is amazing,
but THE CITY’s body is not adapted for VR.

VR Gloves adds a new fixed outfit for 0**₽**, BEAR and USEC at Ragman lvl 1:

-Wearing the topwill turn invisible your arms in 1st person (reworked model).

I improved and optimized the base model while working on it, I also fixed the weight painting so that it doesn’t bend weirdly in VR.

-Wearing the pants will turn invisible your legs both in 1st person and 3rd.

THE CITY displays the same model in 1st and 3rd person, so without modifying how the game works, having no legs means having no legs.

It helps differentiate you from non VR users, lower the chances of teamkills and don’t worry, the bots won’t equip it by default.
The mods works without VR too, is compatible coop (Fika), even between VR and non VR players (if you don’t want it to impact your character simply equip a different uniform).
