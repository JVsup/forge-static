This is my collection of mods, combined into one for easy use and development

Mod page is WIP, just wanted to get 4.0.0 version out the way.

  • Main Mods
  • BushWhacker - Lets you walk through bushes like the old days.
  • GrassCutter - Removes grass for a 90’s viewing experience.
  • MasterKey - Gives you the ability to use a Keycard for every door and lock in Tarkov.
  • TradingPlayerView - (bad naming) this will remove the massive list from your view when hovering over a box trying to sell to a trader and it saying this cannot be sold.
  • SpaceUser - This will give you the ability to buy from the flea by hitting space instead of (y) and will allow you to split ammo stacks aswell.
  • EnvironmentEnjoyer - Takes GrassCutter to the extreme and removes tree’s and bushes.
  • PainkillerDesat - Removes the visual effects of painkillers.
  • Debug Mods (for debug use only)
  • InstantSearch - Gives you the ability to instantly search all containers.
  • FoodWater - Stops your PMC from loosing hydration and energy.
  • LootLoss - Gets rid of loot locally for fast loading times.
  • InventoryViewer - Gives you the ability to View secure containers.
  • GodMode - Self explanatory.
  • CameraShake - Removes the aimpunch from being shot.
  • ThermalMode - Adds the ability to see in thermal.
  • BetterThermalMode - Adds a less noisy version.
  • NightVisionMode - Adds the ability to see in Night vision

Any issues, please report them here https://github.com/CWXDEV/MegaMod/issues
