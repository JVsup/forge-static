Gambler Overview

The Gambler trader sells a variety of configurable mystery boxes that offer new ways to gamble for riches and better player equipment. Gambler specifically offers ways to randomly generate loadouts and gear at the click of a button. All gambling containers except for loadouts are dynamically priced by the Gambler depending on all possible rewards in each mystery box, specific odds for each tier of items in each gamble, and the configurable return of investment each mystery box has over time. Price balancing occurs on server start and can be configured in the config settings of the mod, this is explained further in the trader features section below.

Highly Recommended Mods

  • UI Fixes - Provides ability to set a hot key to open mystery boxes quickly, instead of right clicking every container and pressing ‘unbox’
  • Kaeno Trader Scrolling - If you have too many traders this mod allows you to scroll to reach all of them
  • Live Flea Prices - Mystery box prices will feel much more balanced with this mod. Very Highly recommended!
  • Definitive Weapon Variants - Adds weapon variants to weapon mystery box pool.

Trader Features

Dynamically Priced Mystery Boxes

  • All Mystery Boxes will automatically have its trader sold price dynamically calculated on server startup depending on the price of all rewards in each individual mystery box, mystery box odds for each reward tier, and the expected return of investment that is expected from each gamble. (Each component can be adjusted and changed in the config file.)

Highly Configurable Mystery Boxes:

  • Price:

    • Flea market price of each rewards is used to calculate the final price of each mystery box.

      • Compatible with Live Flea Prices Mod and all price generation will occur after live flea prices is loaded.

    • Each mystery box can be manually priced if you don’t want to use dynamic price generation in the config.

  • Odds:
    • Percentage weights of each reward tier can be adjusted in the config and will play a major factor in the final price.
      • Example: increasing the “keycard_red”: 0.1, value to 10.0 will increase the odds of rewarding a red keycard from 0.1% to 10.0%. Each new gamble with the keycard mystery box will have a 10% to unbox a red keycard. This make the keycard mystery box much more rewarding, and now much more expensive.
  • Expected Return on Investment:
    • Each mystery container has a “_profit_percentage” in the config file which determines how profitable a mystery container should be.
    • Example:
      • If the config has the following: “armor_profit_percentage”: 1.10, The Armor Mystery Box will on average return 110% of your money back. Making your money back and 10% profit.

Current Available Mystery Boxes

Mystery Wallet

  • Gamble your roubles for a chance to win even more roubles or lose it all!
  • The most equal gamble offered by the Gambler. 50% you make money, 50% chance you lose. How far will you go?

Mystery Key

  • Includes all available keys that can be found in THE CITY, ranges from all common keys, uncommon keys, rare keys, and extremely rare keys.

Mystery Keycard

  • Includes all Labs keycards. Offers ability to gamble for a regular access keycard for a little cheaper, a chance for a rarer keycard, or a chance at unboxing nothing.

Mystery Loadout

  • Unboxes a whole raid kit in one unbox. Includes weapon, ammo, gear, meds, and food. Loadout is randomly generated in tiers: scav, mid-wipe, late-wipe, and meme kits. Loadout generation is randomised depending on the tier unboxed and includes a small chance that generation can upgrade or downgrade tiers of all items to increase variety and randomness.

Premium Mystery Loadout

  • Unboxes a late-wipe kit, all random generation can include items from the mid-wipe tier for added variety and randomness

Weapon Mystery Box

  • 1000+ weapons available to be unboxed, Includes Meta weapons, early wipe weapons, late wipe weapons, base weapons, and Meme/cursed weapons

Mystery Ammo

  • Gamble your roubles for a chance at unboxing some rare ammunition, be careful as these can be a money sink, but can be worth it for a chance at some late wipe ammunition.

Mystery 2.5 Million Rouble 50/50

Installation

  1. Remove any old version of the Gambler trader before installing any new update located in SPT/user/mods.
  2. Download the mod, open zip file then drag and drop ‘user’ folder into your SPT directory.
  3. You don’t have to worry about load order anymore as that is handled by the mod now.

Config Features: GamblerTrader\config\config.jsonc

  • Ability to change mystery container prices, stock, and odds of each outcome (Current Odds are subject to change in updates)
  • Profitability settings of each mystery container will directly influence each mystery container price
  • All Mystery containers offer the ability to be disabled if you don’t want the Gambler to offer certain mystery containers

Known Compatibility Issues:

  • Raid Overhaul - Make sure The Gambler loads before Raid Overhaul in the Load Order. The console will spam handbook price issues if not properly loaded before Raid Overhaul

Update 0.2.3 includes updates to loadout generation, major balancing changes, a few new additions to the weapon mystery box, and more…

Loadout Generation

  • Removed chance to receive stock weapons
    • Scav tier weapons have replaced the chance for the original chance of stock weapons, as the scav tier weapon pool has a lot more weapons to generate from in this update

Weapon Mystery Box

  • Added 50 new scav tier weapons
  • Added 12 new semi-modded weapons

Mystery Keycard

Automatic Mystery Container Price Balancer

  • All mystery containers (excluding Mystery Loadout) are now officially price generated by the automatic price balancer.
    • Weapon Mystery Box and Mystery Helmet will calculate the price of the base weapon/helmet and all the included attachments when determining the final container price.
  • Rewrote how containers are price generated. Now all calculations are much more accurate than the previous implementation.
  • Container price generation during server load is now much faster, pretty much instantaneous.

BALANCING:

  • Most Mystery Containers now have infinite trader stock as I’m much more confident with the automatic price balancing as this change allows the player to gamble until they are broke.

  • Re-calibrated overall chance to profit on all containers:

    • With the change of allowing infinite container stock and better automatic price balancing, all containers are set at a fair price to allow the player to gamble to their hearts content. Most containers will be a little more expensive than before, but keep in mind there is always a chance to strike it rich, as everything is a gamble.

  • The only main issue I am having with balancing is the price of blacklisted items that don’t have a finite price that everyone agrees on. This is something that will continue to updated on. Blacklisted item prices like ammo and armour can always be changed in the config at “mystery_container_override_price” if you want to take a look at how I’ve been pricing items that are injected into the automatic price balancer.

Fixes:

  • Fixed mistakenly mixed up weapon mystery box tiers, which created weapons of different tiers than expected.

Update 0.2.0 - Feeling too lazy to gather all the gear you need before loading into a raid? ADDED

  • Loadout Mystery Bag
    • One kit, one unbox. Includes all gear and equipment you need for a raid—all in one mystery box.
    • Items include:
      • Weapon, Extra Magazines, Ammo, Helmet, Armour, Rig, Backpack, Food, Drink, and Medical Supplies
    • Unboxed items are randomly generated and correlate by rarity, meaning if you unbox a meta/highly modded weapon you will have a high chance of generating high end gear and equipment with it. Semi-modded and lower tier weapons will have a higher chance of generating gear of lower rarity as well. Although if you receive a meme/cursed weapon all gear generation will be completely random just for complete meme tier randomness.
    • This feature is still a work in progress and is not perfect. More config options and balancing will appear in future updates, which allow the ability to adjust the loadout odds and items for yourself if you’d like.

    • Added 69 new Weapons:
      • 22 heavily modded weapons
      • 27 semi-modded weapons
      • 13 scav tier weapons
      • 7 meme tier weapons
      • A few existing weapons were redesigned.
  • Mystery Medical Kit
    • The Gambler offers a fresh set of medical supplies, in exchange for a few barter medical items.
  • Mystery Melee
    • Added Kukri to loot pool.

BALANCING

  • Weapon Mystery Box (Buff)
    • Reconfigured the odds for each rarity, Greatly reducing the chance of receiving a base weapon with zero added attachments as these weapons had a really high chance before and were very boring to receive as frequently as they were previously.
  • Mystery Ammo: (Buff)
    • Increased the minimum and maximum amount of ammunition generated for most calibers.
    • Increased chances of receiving rarer ammunition.

  • Mystery Keycard: (Buff)

  • Refactored and overhauled Gambling mechanic and design of Mystery Containers in preparation for the ability to let players be able to create their own mystery containers with the capability of being shared with others. (future update…)

  • Increased speed of mystery container price generation during server startup.

  • Fixed .366 Mystery Ammo price generation from erroring out and becoming a default price.
