MedEffectsHUD

Tired of opening your profile every 10 seconds to check your buffs? No idea how much time is left on your painkiller? Zagustin wore off mid-fight and you didn't even notice? Missed the moment your endurance stim expired? Never again.

Description Versions 6

A compact in-raid HUD overlay — all active buffs and debuffs right on your screen, with timers and values.

What it shows

  • All buffs from stimulators, meds, and skills — with countdown timers
  • All debuffs — bleedings, fractures, tremor, pain — nothing slips by
  • Time remaining for every effect
  • Blinking warning when a buff is about to expire
  • Works on every localizations.

Fully customizable

  • ICONS! Can customise icons via chanching it in folder of mod. (u can change them if u want to on this folder BepInEx\plugins\MedEffectsHUD\icons - there is some overridding icons in positive and negative buffs so watch them too. Size of icons needs to be 32x32 and PNG ONLY! DO NOT RENAME ICON NAMES!)
  • Colors for buff names, debuff names, values, and timers — all configurable
  • 3 layout modes: side-by-side columns or stacked list, Icons only
  • Auto-abbreviated long names, sorted by time remaining
  • Blacklist — hide effects you don’t care about
  • Position, size, font, transparency — make it yours
  • Notification: new debuffs or expiring buffs on middle of the screen (configurable)

Toggle HUD with F8 by default (configurable).

Installation

  1. Download the MedEffectsHUD.zip
  2. Extract it into the root folder of your SPT installation
  3. Folders merge automatically — if prompted to overwrite, say yes
  4. Launch the game — press F8 to toggle the HUD
