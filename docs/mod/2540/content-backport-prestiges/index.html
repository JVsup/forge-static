What’s included

‘Content Backport - Prestiges’ restores the missing prestiges from 1.0, this mod will allow you to prestige all the way to Prestige 6 (and receive the correct rewards for each prestige)

What this mod does, specifically:

  • Changes prestige 1 to 4 to have the new requirements added by BSG in 1.0.5.0
  • Adds Prestige 5 and Prestige 6 to the game, as well as all the relevant images and client assets that are required for it (If they weren’t included in WTT’s Content Backport already)
  • Adds the missing achievements for leveling up in prestige, you will now correctly receive the achievements for each prestige level (And also if your profile has prestiged past level 1 and never got any of the achievements!)
  • Adds the missing requirements to the Collector quest, namely for the new streamer items
  • Adds the missing ‘New Beginnings’ quests that are required on each prestige level.
  • Removes the cheat code to be able to unlock the new prestige rewards (Sorry, you filthy cheaters :))

What’s missing?

  • Nothing, fully updated since July 2026 :)
