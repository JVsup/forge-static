FEATURES

Balances armor classes, durabilities, damage, penetration, and other ballistic qualities to play well with my other mods. This is by no means an improvement on the REALISM aspect, rather a deliberate tweak to add purpose to many otherwise useless items and further spread the strength of items along the value and rarity spectrum.
