Disable Headshot Protect

Want to die from getting shot in the head? Time to fix another jank BSG design decision

This client modification reverts BSG’s decision to allow certain bots on certain ranges to essentially not shoot you in the head by simply ignoring the damage received on your player, all of the times you saw blood splatters and took no damage is likely due to this.

Not compatible with SAIN, as I have already added this fix to SAIN in 4.4.0
