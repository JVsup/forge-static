Air Filter Only Drains In Raid

Are you tired of feeling bad about your air filters draining while you’re busy managing your inventory or doing stuff in your hideout? Well no more!

With this mod the air filters in your hideout Air Filtering Unit will only drain while you’re actually in a PMC raid! (Not in Scav raids)

This means the air filter timer in your hideout now shows how long you can spend inside raids before it runs out.

Balance

This mod doesn’t change how long an air filter lasts so if you want to balance it I recommend using SVM to set your air filter drain rate to somewhere around 1.5 to 2.

FIKA Compatibility

I made the mod to track in-raid status per session ID. Users of this mod have confirmed it works as intended with FIKA.

Known Issues

If you ALT+F4 or crash during a PMC raid your air filter will keep draining until you start your client again or stop the server. This is because there is no straightforward server-side way to track in-raid PMCs.
