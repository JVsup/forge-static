Hello, Im Champion Of Light and this addon is an expansion of my camos which got into the mod “Weapon Camo and Stickers’. This addon might be not big as I wanted, but it adds more variety of customization for your weapon and serves for me as a first attempt to post something here.

Later Ill try to post screenshots

Mod Includes:

8 new camos

5 new masks like Berezka, tree sticks and etc

13 new stickers which is based on ancient nordic, paganic symbols and runes All things that were added are trying to be in military style without any really bright stuff
