DISCLAIMER: THIS PRESET IS MEANT TO BE USED IN CONJUCTION WITH ORBIT

(due to SAIN extracting being disabled, thus letting ORBIT handle bot extraction.)

I made this preset with the goal of making my personal experience on SPT as intense but also infuriating as possible. And what I mean by that is… killing a squad of PMCs who will laser you if you aren’t quick and accurate will feel satisfying. But if you mess up, you WILL die, A LOT. If you’re a borderline masochist like me, and like basically torturing yourself on THE CITY but without the problems that Live THE CITY brings, this preset is for you. But if you aren’t looking for that kind of thing, that’s okay, this preset just isn’t for you.

In order to get the intended experience, I’d suggest making sure all PMC bots are set to Impossible difficulty.

Installation

  1. Make sure you have SAIN installed
  2. Download the add-on and drag the “BepInEx” folder into your SPT installation folder (same one as the Launcher and Server).
Some of the changes made to make the AI a lot more difficult are (but not limited to, since I forget all the things that were tweaked):
  • Much faster reactions
    • CQB reactions are on for all bot types that SAIN controls, minimum reaction speeds are reduced but not impossible to deal with, if you repeek an angle, expect to get shot before you even see the bot, best to reposition and never repeek the same angle twice.
  • Higher overall accuracy, MUCH less random scatter, CoM is closer to head.
    • Bots will be very accurate very quickly after starting to shoot globally, Precision Speed Multiplier is increased to 1.25. Accuracy Speed set to 0.75, which makes their shots more accurate over time, just quicker to do so. Scatter Multiplier is set to 0.35, this setting is what has the most effect in them being very deadly. Center of Mass is set to 0.225, which is around the upper chest, which reduces the chance of getting a blacked stomach, but will also increase the chance of getting headshot due to random scatter and recoil.
There is also some other changes made to make the AI more balanced but not insanely difficult to where it’s not fun with their increased accuracy and overall deadliness:
  • Hearing randomization increased, and ranges reduced in these categories:
    • Increased the randomization at which sound location pinpointing operates, thus reducing their accuracy in finding exactly where a sound came from.
    • Surgery Usage, I reduced this to around 35 meters from the default of (I think) 70 meters, makes it less likely to get rushed by a nearby bot when doing surgery after a fight.
    • Healing, Reduced to 35 meters as well.
    • Aiming Down Sights, reduced to 25 meters from 35 meters, not a huge difference, but could make the difference when being stealthy.

I have long felt that the AI hearing is wayyyy to good and will basically lock onto you through walls, effectively giving them ESP. And from my personal experience, these changes help to alleviate that somewhat. Keep in mind bots still can be pinpoint accurate in locating exactly where you are with the more sound you make. So results may vary.

I probably missed a bunch of other changes made to the preset, but I’ve been tweaking this for like 5 months and didn’t ever think to document my changes. Anyways, I hope you enjoy this SAIN Preset, I wasn’t originally going to make the preset public but I had gotten some requests to do so, and I thought “Why not? Maybe some people would enjoy it like me.”
