Harmony's Hardcore Rules

Flea market got shut down and traders no longer treat you nice - what do you do?

Description Versions 1

This my personal preset for SVM I use, so, feel free to use it too!

Key changes:

  • No surgical meds in secure containers - they are only allowed inside special slots.
  • No ammo in secure containers.
  • No flea (actually, unlocks at level 55).
  • Increased prices for almost everything - reduced free healing from Therapist, x1.8 multiplier for all trader prices, less items at Fence (more at crown), BTR, Taxi, etc.
  • Less chance for insurance to return, along with the weapon/armor mods.
  • And other stuff that’d make your progression harder, but not too annoying

Installation

  1. Drag and drop the zip contents in your root game directory.
  2. Open Greed.exe and down below load the custom preset (Make sure to click “Load Preset”).
  3. Click “Apply” at the bottom left corner of Greed app.
  4. Profit!

Warning

If you had any crucial changes made on your profile before SVM, I highly suggest to NOT use this Preset for your own sake of not breaking the profile. This preset sets backpack restrictions lower, and defaults all the stash spaces. UNEXPECTED THINGS CAN HAPPEN, WHICH MAY BREAK YOUR PROFILE IF HAD SVM CHANGES BEFORE!
