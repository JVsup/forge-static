Matts Better Boolets and Booms

This is a simple add-on that adds new boolets and grenades, but keeps them balanced so there is no break in progression or difficulty. This now has restricted Arena Ammo. Another major feature of this Add-on has become a total rebalance of shotgun shells.

General Purpose

This mod is aimed at filling in gaps in ammo and helping some weapons feel a bit better to use. This is not a hyper ammo or depleted uranium mod. Those are fun, nothing is wrong with them. But that’s not what I want, and maybe there are some that agree. Basically, I wanted to make less loved calibers be more viable for general use. Within reason.

My huge thanks to Ammogen and its creator Shaneeexd. I have wanted to do something like this for awhile. Their tool allowed me to do exactly what I aimed at doing.

If anyone has a suggestion, please leave a comment. My goal is balance, fun, and gameplay synergy. If you think something is too strong or too weak, please leave a comment.

Compatibility: I play with many of the standards like WTT armory, 1.0 Backport, and a few others. Ammogen is a remarkable tool, and appears to have been able to make the ammos work right outta the box. (Buh dum tis)

Crafting: I have included crafts for some of the ammos in this pack. The crafts are mostly sane, with only a little bit of THE CITY-y jank in their recipes. Again, comment any suggestions you have.

Installation: Install Ammogen as directed, then install this addon the same way. You can also manually add the json file into the Ammogen Ammo file.

Use: Feel free to use and modify this pack for your own enjoyment. My only ask is that you not distribute it and give credit to me and Shaneexd when applicable.

Version 1.7.0 6.5 CBJ and APIs for 7.62 NATO and 7.62 R

The 6.5 CBJ is a super exciting and relatively new derivation of 9mm. The cartridge takes up the same dimensions as 9mm, but features a necked down casing, similar to .357 SIG, but from 9mm to 6.5mm. This enables much higher velocities, sometimes in excess of 900 mps (2950 fps) out of a 12 inch barrel. From an 8 inch barrel, velocities around 830 mps (2724 fps) are regular. From a pistol length barrel (4.5-5 inches) velocities remain high at around 730 m/s (2350 fps).

APDS - This cartridge is capable of frankly incredible armor penetration. Its “Core” cartridge is a 4mm pure tungsten penetrator surrounded by a 6.5mm sabot. It can penetrate 9mm of hardened steel armor. Watch the Forgotten Weapons video on it. Wild stuff from a pistol too!

HET - This particular cartridge is a 2.5 gram brass semi-armor piercing projectile. Its purpose is for law enforcement to have a poor ballistic coefficient, limiting its range and chance for third party injuries during shooting events. However, its penetration is still remarkable, able to penetrate a kevlar and titanium target and maintain 12 inches of soft target penetration. Its wound channel is also notable.

Subsonic - This particular cartridge is an 8 gram subsonic projectile with a tungsten penetrator core. The result is a subsonic cartridge that can still match the NATO CRISAT requirements, effectively penetrating level 3 (in THE CITY) armor while still penetrating over 12 inches in gel.

HP - This particular cartridge is a solid Copper expanding hollow point similar to many personal defense rounds on the market. However this round is screaming at over 2700 fps, making rapid and energetic expansion guaranteed. It still maintains incredible penetration for a pistol hollowpoint.

Frangible - This particular cartridge is a 2.5 gram copper-tin sinstered projectile. Aimed at use in training and high security areas like power plants and planes where damaging the surrounding equipment is costly and dangerous. This round disintegrates upon contact with a hard surface, but perform remarkably well with soft targets.

APIs: Armor piercing incendiary with a small explosive: 7.62x51mm M14A1 - This round was designed as an Armor Piercing Incendiary (API) round intended for the 7.62 NATO platforms. It features a 148 grain projectile with about 7-8 grains of incendiary powder in front of a steel penetrator.

The 7.62x54mm R ZBZ3 - This is a soviet and russian designed Armor Piercing Incendiary (API) round intended for the SVD/SVDS platforms. It features a 151 grain projectile with about 9-11 grains of incendiary powder in front of a steel penetrator.

Revised and refined all armor damage percentages. I slightly misunderstood what the values represented, some were too high, some too low. Should be more progressive throughout.

So, now it has a much more niche role of almost a breaching slug. Tried it in a few raids today and was having a lot of fun. Still expensive, but it is a 28 pen high damage slug after all. Other armor penetrating slugs will perform better, but this has a nice close in role now.

Changes to penetration of all 3.5 inch magnum shotgun shells. Also changes to certain recoil and accuracy stats. General mixed balancing of a few others.

Version 1.6.0 Restricted Ammunition

I have been toying around with creating shells and bullets that can only be used in specific weapons or categories of weapons. It took some time and effort, but I think I have done it. These certainly need testing, so let me know if something is off.

I did two categories to start off. First is pump shotgun shells, and second is high penetration bolt action rifle rounds. (Arena ammo, lets be real here)

Some final General notes on these: Bring a sidearm. Seriously. These are incredibly high powered and pressured ammos. They are not intended for extended combat, but rather niche burst damage. I believe I restricted most weapons you can use these in, but there might be a way to sneak a shell or round into a semi auto or god forbid an auto weapon. PLEASE DONT. not only is that kinda immersion breaking, it will be a bad time. I set the durability burn to be very high, these are crazy rounds. BUT, I also set the heat generation to be truly absurd. The low heat generation and cyclic rate of pump actions and bolt actions means that in those systems, the heat really isn’t a problem. But in a semiauto, good luck getting through a single mag or a tube and a half of shells. If this ends up being over tuned, let me know. But the whole point is to limit these to pumps and bolts.

Version 1.5.0 The great shotgun balancing act

So a little explanation first; Big Silly Goose shotgun shells are so oddly balanced and make very little sense to me. Pretty much every shell has 8 or 9 pellets, with negligible difference in performance to the degree that pretty much there are two viable options, 5.25mm and Magnum buck. 6.5mm is also an option, but a single pellet cannot headshot, to me that greatly limits its usefulness when 5.25mm can headshot for quite a distance and is cheaper. Also, 7mm is just awful. It should perform only second to magnum, but is by far and above the worst option. The ballistic coefficient is needlessly terrible, and for some reason they gave each pellet ( which is the same size as #2 buckshot btw) the same weight as #7 birdshot. I have no words. So rant over, what did I do?

I standardized, balanced, and diversified the shotgun ammo choices. I pretty much remade every base EFT shell into a real world (mostly) example that is balanced and fun. Version 1.2

9x21mm FR gzh: A leg meta special feature. High flesh damage, virtually no pen. However, the extra powder needed to propel the bullet fasted for more hydrostatic shock means more heat. A good bit more.

4.6x30mm FRANG SX: Another leg meta gift. Basically I just made a 4.6 version of the 5.7x28mm R37 F. This is a high damage, low pen round for early game and high RoF guns.

KS-23: The KS-23 shells may be the most difficult shells I have made yet. At first I went into this thinking they would be easy, Big Soviet Gun Stronk Comrade. But I quickly learned how wrong peoples perception of this gun and similar guns is. This is not a crazy soviet era 23mm super shotgun. Its a utilitarian multitool for police and specialty military units. It usually uses stun rounds, gas grenade rounds, and the flash bang. The slug is used purely to stop cars and shoot through light barricades. The shrapnel shells were almost afterthoughts. Hell, the Shrapnel 25 is pretty much a 2 ¾ inch standard 00 buckshot round adapted to fit in the 23mm shell. The system is so low pressure, it really cant push the shells at any real velocity. Honestly, the in game rounds are too powerful for the real deal. The civilian market created much better and more effective shells. Quick note: most civilian shells were for the TOS-123, which was actually a smoothbore 26mm gun! I think they basically bored out the rifled AA 23mm barrels to make them. Most sources, of which there are few btw, estimate the civilian shells to be about on par with a 10 gauge to possible a 10 gauge magnum in power and capacity. So the three shells I added are pretty much in line with those, except I slightly reduced the velocity. Again, the KS-23 was quite low pressure, getting 1.5-2 ounce payloads to 1400 fps is gonna grenade the thing. For reference, here is a picture f the cross sections for the rounds. https://imgur.com/rgh2Xr7

23x75 “Shrapnel 5” Buckshot: Reload a used KS-23 shell with #4 buckshot and you have this shell. 54 pellets of #4 buck flung at barely supersonic speeds. Fun times.

23x75 Steel Birdshot: 144 pellets of BB birdshot at roughly the speed of sound is a recipe for a bad time on the receiving end. Will for sure cause a whole heap of bleeds assuming they survive the first shot. Super wide spread, very short range.

23x74mm Lead Slug: The civilian smoothbore versions rarely used slugs, but when they did it was a big ass hunk of lead launched as fast as possible. Believe it or not the Barrakada slug the military used was actually only about a .50 cal sized bullet at 500 grains. .50 BMG is a much better engine stopper. But the barakada basically can’t over penetrate, where as .50 BMG and the russian equivalent absolutely would. Thus, a 2 ounce lead slug (875 grains) being much heavier than 500, this is going to go slower. Another interesting thing, Big Silly Goose coded the Barakada to be 80 grams, or over 1200 grains. That is just absurd. The Ks-23 would never handle shooting that at 420 m/s.

Minor changes: AP-21: Reduced price, moved to Peacekeeper 3, reduced pen, increased damage, increased bleeding, increased accuracy, reduced armor damage, reduced speed slightly, greatly increased recoil and heat/dura burn. Now fulfills a slightly earlier game niche. Revised description. Price and minor stat adjustments.

Version 1.1.1 Version 1.1.0 was a failed attempt to properly upload this to github. I’m still learning 4.6, 5.6. SIG, .300 blackout, .357, and .50 AE

6.8x51mm SIG HP - Available at peacekeeper 1, this high flesh damage low pen ammo is perfect for early game. Plus with the high ROF of the SPEAR, it can even be useful late game if your short on cash but just happen to have a spear on hand. Also its funny.

6.8x51mm SIG Brass Hunter - Available at peacekeeper level 2, this lower pen ammo allows low level players to use the spear and still do well.

6.8x51 SIG Hybrid Black Tip - OH yeah, M993 at home. This is a late game ammo, only available in raid or through lightkeeper in any reasonable quantity. Peacekeeper sells a very small quantity at a huge markup. Not much else to say.

.300 Blackout Punisher - Based on a real life frangible copper ammo, this nasty little devil breaks in to 3 leaves and a center base upon impact. Look up the wound cavities, definitely a war crime. But this is THE CITY! Roast those shins! .357 Magnum Copper penetrator - Xtreme penetrator. Better pen than fmj, less damage, no fragmentation chance. Pretty simple. Can black arms at close range, but not legs. Should make the chiappa and especially the .357 deagle more appealing to use early to mid game.

.50 AE Frangible Hollowpoint - A copper tin hybrid HP designed to dump its energy as fast as possible. Lots of bleeding chance. Pretty much no armor pen. Very useful if you dump a few rounds downrange and retreat, as the target will certainly have one or two light bleeds and probably a heavy one too. Up close, dump a mag into their pelvis and they will crumple over.

.50 AE Xtreme Penetrator - Yup, here we go again. This one offers marginally better pen over FMJ, but losses some of the important 1 shot potential. Can no longer 1 tap the chest, but can black the stomach at close range. Useful to ensure helmet pens, or reliably 2 tap the thorax. And of course the butt clinching mag dumps will be quite effective.

4.6x28mm AP GX - Take a small piece of tungsten, add it to the rear to increase weight and likelihood of tumbling. Sounds like something Mechanic would do right? Oddly enough this is a downgrade over the top tier ammo. This on has less pen, but more damage. This fills a niche roll that 4.6 left open. AP SX has a hilarious pen of 53, but damage is exactly 35. Meaning any range or any damage reduction from armor pen, and you can no longer 1 tap the head. AP GX has 49 pen, and 39 damage. So it has a much longer 1 tap range. This makes for a cool synergy where the worse pen ammo gets front loaded in the mag to get reliable 1 taps, but the higher pen stuff goes after to shred the giga chads. I really like this one, let me know what you think.

5.7x30mm SS90 - Your read that right, SS90. This was the original round HK intended to use. It was a super long and light round moving at stupid speed. 23 grains (1.5 grams) at 880 m/s. Silly stuff. But it just didn’t do well in penetration. I added this round as a rare find. It doesn’t really fill a niche, but i think its a cool piece or firearm history/lore.

5.7x30mm SS190 X - Put a small tungsten penetrator cap and a little extra powder behind it and you gain some AP SX pen, with AP GX damage. The downside is much higher durability burn and heating generation. So a full mag of this stuff will fold you durability and might even jam on the 1st mag. But if you top load it for a few, and then put normal 190, you get a nice combo. Expensive, and rare. Very rare.
