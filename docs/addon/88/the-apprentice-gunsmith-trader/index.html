The Apprentice — Gunsmith Trader

Mechanic's former apprentice sells every Gunsmith contract weapon pre-assembled. No workbench, no parts hunting — unlocked through a four-quest storyline.

PraetorianGuard
Downloads
64
Description Versions 2

Nikolai “Kolya” Varga spent four years at Mechanic’s bench before walking out and taking the skill with him. He builds the same rifles his old master asks for — finished, measured, sitting on a rack.

You pay, you walk out, and Mechanic accepts them without a word.

What it adds

26 pre-assembled weapons covering Gunsmith Part 1–25, including the Part 21 M700 / M1911 pair. Every build follows the publicly documented contract configuration and has been verified in-game.

A four-quest storyline that gates his stock — the racks stay empty until you prove yourself.

Quest Level Unlocks
The Easy Way 5 Loyalty 1
Supply Lines 12 Loyalty 2
Quality Control 18 Loyalty 3
Old Debts 24 Loyalty 4

Each quest awards trader standing, so progression follows the storyline rather than player level alone. The thresholds work on every EFT edition.

Trader

  • Stock: 1 of each weapon, restocks every 30–60 minutes
  • Buys weapons, mods, repair kits and tools — pays marginally better than Mechanic at higher loyalty
  • Repairs weapons
  • Not available on the flea market

Requirements

  • SPT 4.0.13
  • TraderGen 2.0.3+
  • WTT - CommonLib 2.0.20+

Notes

Weapon builds follow the publicly documented Gunsmith configurations. Because SPT runs one patch behind live EFT, individual attachment stats may differ; if a contract is not accepted, adjust the build.

Fixed five builds that were rejected in-game. Published parts lists only cover attachments to buy, not stock parts that have to come off.

  • Part 5 (M870) — removed XS SHOTRAIL rail and EOTech XPS3-2 sight
  • Part 18 (AKMN) — removed NSPU-M night vision scope and eyecup
  • Part 20 (M1A) — 22 inch barrel swapped for 16 inch with SOCOM 16 muzzle brake
  • Part 21 (M700) — removed bipod, MDT 12-round magazine swapped for PMAG AC 5-round
  • Part 24 (SR-25) — removed bipod mount and AN/PEQ-15 tactical device

The remaining 21 builds were verified in-game and accepted without changes. Prices recalculated from handbook values.
